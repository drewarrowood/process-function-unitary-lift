Claim

Shown: if a process function sends two distinct output strings, some party disagrees in the output and agrees in the function value. On bits the source-and-sink map is a bijection. Written, not kernel-checked: the gram identity. Not claimed: a metric for Lugano.

o = f(w(o)).

w(a, b, c) = (not b and c, not c and a, not a and b).

(o, e) maps to (w(o) XOR e, o). The inverse sends (i, s) to (s, i XOR w(s)).

Both compositions are the identity on bits. That is the check in Full.lean. The shift is delta_k = w_k(s') XOR w_k(s). The witness party kills the off-diagonal. So M M-dagger = I, written, not kernel-checked.
