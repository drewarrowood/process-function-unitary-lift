Unique completion, the unitary lift, and the embeddability gap

Lean. lean/Full.lean has the exclusivity lemma and the source-and-sink bijection on bits. Lean 4.34.1 accepted both, with no unfinished proof. The gram identity is not in that file.

Shown. Distinct output strings leave a party who disagrees in the output and agrees in the function value. The bit extension is a bijection.

Written. That witness kills the off-diagonal, so the induced map is unitary.

Not claimed. A metric. A wormhole Hamiltonian for Lugano. The Leifer-Pusey no-go. Freedom.

1. The demand

Faye's Stanford entry, revised 28 October 2025, still treats the consistency paradoxes as unresolved. A logic of backward causation has to block grandfather cases and bootstrap cases without collapsing into the stipulation that only consistent loops occur. Dummett, in 1954 and 1964, set the lexical objection aside. A stipulation about the word cause is not a discovery. Black, in 1956, asked what happens to the intervention. Lewis, in 1976, split the modal and did not select the histories. Horwich asked the theory to say what the intervention does. Earman said a filter imposed after the dynamics is not a dynamics.

A process function answers by non-admission. An intervention is a tuple of local functions. The map is admitted only when the composition has one fixed point. No fixed point is the grandfather case. Two is the bootstrap. The identity loop fails the clause. The swap fails it. A rider that assigns inconsistent solutions measure zero cannot be failed from inside the theory in that way.

2. Spacetime, and three quantum replies

Godel's 1949 solution has closed timelike curves. Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever convert a traversable wormhole into a time machine if the throat stays open. Echeverria, Klinkhammer and Thorne found billiard data with one continuation, two, or none. A process function wants multiplicity one for every intervention. The billiard does not supply it.

Deutsch asks for a fixed point of the partial trace. Existence is a theorem. Uniqueness is not. Lloyd and collaborators replace the fixed point by post-selection. Process functions sit inside that simulation. Oreshkov, Costa and Brukner drop global order. The quantum switch does not violate a causal inequality. Lugano does.

3. The equations

o = f(w(o)). (1)

The fixed point is in the output. No solution is the grandfather case. Two solutions is the bootstrap.

w(a, b, c) = (not b and c, not c and a, not a and b). (2)

Lugano. Each input depends on the other two outputs. All 64 binary interventions have one solution of (1). The image has four elements out of eight, so (2) is two-to-one.

U sends (o, e) to (w(o) + e, o). (3)

The inverse sends (i, s) to (s, i + w(s)). (4)

On bits the sum is exclusive or, so (4) after (3) is the identity. The Lean file checks both compositions. A finite bijection is a permutation unitary. That is representation, not yet a quantum process. The extra coordinates are operational boundaries, not a throat.

If s differs from s', some party disagrees in the output and agrees in the function value. (5)

Otherwise one local function fixes both strings in (1). The swap fails (5): negation on each side fixes both (0, 1) and (1, 0). This is process_disagreement in the Lean file.

delta_k = w_k(s') - w_k(s). (6)

MM dagger = I. (7)

The amplitude is a product because each unitary sees only its own symbol. The shift is the only place the classical function enters the contraction. On the diagonal it vanishes. Off it, (5) supplies a party with shift zero and distinct rows, so that factor is the inner product of two distinct rows and vanishes. Equation (7) is written, not kernel-checked. A local instrument is a unitary on an ancilla followed by a partial trace. Fixing the source at 0 in (3) recovers w.

i_B = w(o_A), and i_A does not depend on o_B. (8)

Equation (8) is a channel. It embeds as free propagation. No wormhole is required. Equation (2) has no such wiring if the settings are free. Post-selection is a simulation. Equations (3) and (4) are reversibility, not a metric. The billiard has the wrong multiplicity. The cyclic case stays open.

4. What would be new

Araujo, Feix, Navascues and Brukner quantized (2) in 2017. The 2019 paper proves (3) classically and leaves the quantum step open. Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal proved a sufficient condition this sketch does not use. The new step is that (5) is the off-diagonal. The prohibition is checked. The identification is written. Unique completion answers the logical half of the demand in Faye's entry. It does not answer the geometric half.
