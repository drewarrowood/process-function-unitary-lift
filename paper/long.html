Every process function lifts to a unitary process

A longer note, with the proof sketched in full and with the boundary of the claim marked where it changes.

Abstract

A process function is a classical map from the outputs of several parties to their inputs such that every local deterministic intervention has exactly one fixed point. Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych proved that every such map extends to a bijection by a source and a sink, and left open whether the bijection quantizes to a valid unitary process. Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal conjectured that it does, and proved a sufficient condition that is not necessary. This note sketches a direct argument: the dilation is that bijection, read as a permutation unitary, and the process-function clause is the cancellation in the source-to-sink gram matrix. The combinatorial half is kernel-checked. The identification with complex local unitaries is written and not kernel-checked. Embeddability of the cyclic case in a spacetime is not claimed.

1. What is shown, what is sketched, what is refused

Shown, in Lean 4.34.1, with no unfinished proof. If a process function on a finite alphabet sends two distinct output strings, some party disagrees in the output symbol and agrees in the function value. On bits, the source-and-sink map is a bijection, and both compositions with the inverse are the identity. The Lugano function has one fixed point for every binary intervention. The identity loop and the three-cycle do not.

Sketched, and not kernel-checked. Contract that permutation unitary with a local unitary at each party, possibly on an ancilla. The gram matrix of the induced source-to-sink map factors across parties. On the diagonal every shift vanishes. Off the diagonal the lemma supplies a party whose shift vanishes and whose rows differ, so that factor is the inner product of two distinct rows and is zero. Instruments follow by Stinespring. This is the step that would close the 2019 question.

Refused. Nothing in the cancellation is a metric. Ordered process functions embed as channels. The cyclic case has a post-selected simulation, a reversible extension, and no known local Hamiltonian whose solutions are exactly its fixed points.

2. The problem the sketch is aimed at

Backward causation is asked to be consistent, falsifiable, reversible, and quantum. The four demands are usually run together. They are not the same demand.

Consistency is the empty case and the overfull case. An intervention with no fixed point is the grandfather paradox. An intervention with two is the bootstrap. Novikov's principle refuses the first after the fact and leaves the second as a multiplicity. A uniqueness clause blocks both. It can be failed: the identity loop fails it, and the swap fails it. That is the gain over a stipulation that only consistent loops occur. Faye's Stanford entry, revised on 28 October 2025, still treats the demand as open. The process-function clause is a finite-alphabet answer to it, and only to it.

Reversibility is separate. Lugano is two-to-one on its alphabet. A time-symmetric reading that insists the original map be an involution has already refused the witness. The source and the sink restore a bijection without that refusal. They are a boundary. They are not a direction smuggled into the three bits.

The quantum demand is that instruments, not only Boolean functions, have a joint probability. A Boolean fixed point does not give that. The sketch is the claim that the same clause which blocked the double fixed point also kills the off-diagonal of the gram matrix.

3. Bilking, as a constraint on solutions

Dummett, in 1954 and again in 1964, treated the dictionary objection as a stipulation about the word. One may reserve cause for the earlier relatum. The question that remains is whether a later event can be necessary for an earlier one.

Black's bilking argument is the serious one. An agent who sees the earlier event can try to prevent the later one. Either the agent cannot, which wants an explanation that is not itself the paradox, or the later event was not necessary. Lewis splits the modal. One can, relative to the local facts and the laws. One cannot, relative to the whole past. The split dissolves a verbal paradox. It does not select the histories.

The process-function clause selects them. An intervention is a local function from a party's input to that party's output. The global map is admitted only if the composition has one fixed point. Bilking is not forbidden as an action. It is constrained as a solution. That is the no-new-physics principle of Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych: an operation possible in an ordinary region remains possible. Consistency constrains the loop, not the laboratory. The clause can be failed from inside the theory, by exhibiting an intervention with the wrong number of fixed points. A rider that assigns inconsistent solutions measure zero cannot be failed in that way.

4. The billiard, and why it is not this object

Godel's 1949 solution has closed timelike curves. Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever showed in 1988 that a traversable wormhole, held open, can be converted into a time machine by relative motion of the mouths. Friedman and collaborators proposed in 1990 that only self-consistent solutions occur. Echeverria, Klinkhammer and Thorne integrated the hard-sphere billiard on that wormhole. Some data have one consistent continuation, some have two, some have none apparent. Carlini, Frolov, Mensky, Novikov and Solodukhin recovered the same underdetermination from stationarity of the action.

Earman's warning is that a consistency condition imposed after the dynamics is not a dynamics. The billiard is the example. It is a local Hamiltonian, and it does not have one solution for every boundary condition. A process function requires that. The billiard is therefore not an embedding of the Lugano function. It is a counterexample to the hope that the standard wormhole already is one. A unitary dilation of a process function does not repair the billiard. It is a different object, and the sketch below is a sketch of that object, not of a metric.

5. Three quantum replies, and which one this is

Deutsch asks for a fixed point of the partial trace around the curve. In finite dimension one exists, so no action is forbidden, at the cost of nonlinearity and of cloning. Aaronson and Watrous put the computational power at PSPACE. Bennett, Leung, Smith and Smolin called the power an artefact of the fixed-point demand. Tolksdorf and Verch showed that the Deutsch condition can be met to arbitrary precision on a spacetime with no closed timelike curve. A Deutsch fixed point is not diagnostic of a loop, and it is not linear in the instruments. The object in the sketch is linear.

Lloyd, Maccone and collaborators replace the fixed point by post-selection. Process functions sit inside that class. The event list appears in the runs that survive. The probability of success is not one. An embedding would not have a rejected branch. The sketch is not a post-selection. It is a unitary on a larger space, with the source fixed and the sink read out.

Oreshkov, Costa and Brukner define a process as a map from local instruments to probabilities, with no global causal order assumed. The quantum switch of Chiribella, D'Ariano, Perinotti and Valiron is causally nonseparable and does not violate a causal inequality. The Lugano process violates one. The two phenomena are not the same. A unitary process, in the sense of this note, is the Choi operator of the source-and-sink unitary. It is operational. It is not a solution of Einstein's equation.

6. Process functions, enough to state the sketch

Parties have finite alphabets, written additively when a sum is needed. Bits under exclusive or are the case the kernel checked. A process function w sends an output string to an input string. An intervention is a tuple of local functions from a party's input to that party's output. The fixed point is an output string o such that o equals the intervention applied to w(o). The map is a process function when every intervention has exactly one such string.

For two parties the condition forces a causal order. Of the 256 binary maps, twelve are process functions, and none is a cycle. Four have no signalling, four signal one way, four the other. Baumeler and Wolf proved that a classical violation of a causal inequality needs three parties. Every two-party process function is therefore a channel.

The Lugano function is the three-party witness. On bits, the three inputs are the conjunction of the negation of one output and the remaining output, cycled. Every deterministic intervention has one fixed point. The image has four elements out of eight. Reversibility is not free on the original alphabet. That is the fact the source and the sink repair.

Dourdent, Leitherer, Boghiu, Simonov, Kunjwal and Acin restate the clause as a complete and pairwise exclusive event list, a multipartite form of Specker's principle. Completeness and pairwise exclusivity are the unique fixed point, read as a list. The restatement does not mention a metric. The freedom in the clause is the no-new-physics principle: any operation possible in an ordinary region remains possible. Consistency may not forbid the operation. It may only constrain the global solution.

7. Sketch of the proof

The sketch has three steps. The first two are theorems. The third is the written contraction.

Step 1. The disagreement lemma. Let w be a process function, and let s and s' be output strings with s different from s'. Then some party k has s_k different from s'_k and w_k(s) equal to w_k(s').

Suppose not. Then, at every party, agreement of the function values already forces agreement of the outputs. Define a local function that sends w_k(s) to s_k and w_k(s') to s'_k, and send every other symbol anywhere. The two clauses do not conflict, because a conflict would be a symbol that is the function value of both strings while the outputs differ, which is the case just excluded. Both strings are then fixed points of one intervention: the intervention applied to w(s) returns s, and applied to w(s') returns s'. A process function cannot have that. So some party is a witness.

The swap is the witness in the other direction. The strings (0,1) and (1,0), under the map that exchanges its arguments, are both fixed by negation on each side. The identity loop fails for the same reason. The lemma says why, in a form the contraction can use. Lean accepted the construction for bits and for a general finite alphabet, with the fixed point taken in the output.

Step 2. The extension is a bijection. Add a source string e and read a sink string s. Send the pair (o, e) to the pair (w(o)+e, o). The inverse sends (i, s) to (s, i+w(s)), up to the sign convention for the group law. On bits the sum is exclusive or, and exclusive or cancels, so both compositions are the identity. Lean accepted both. A bijection of a finite set is a permutation unitary on the computational basis. That is all the kernel says about unitarity. The 2019 paper proves the classical extension in general. The kernel check is the bit case, with the inverse written down.

Step 3. The contraction. Let U be the permutation unitary of step 2. Put a local unitary V_k at each party, acting on that party's system and, if one likes, on a local ancilla that begins and ends in a fixed state, or is traced at the end. The source-to-sink matrix element is the product, over parties, of the amplitude for V_k to take the shifted source symbol w_k(o)+e_k to the sink symbol s_k. Change variables by the party's source symbol equal to w_k(o)+e_k. The sum over the output string then factors across parties. The entry of M M-dagger between output strings s and s' is a product, over k, of the overlap between row s_k of V_k and row s'_k shifted by w_k(s')-w_k(s).

If s equals s', every shift vanishes and each factor is a row norm, hence 1. If s differs from s', step 1 supplies a party with shift zero and distinct row indices. That factor is the inner product of two distinct rows of a unitary, ancilla included, and vanishes. One zero factor kills the product. So M M-dagger is the identity. The induced map is unitary.

A local instrument is a unitary on a local ancilla followed by a partial trace, by Stinespring. The partial trace of a unitary dilation is completely positive and trace-preserving. Fixing the source at the identity element of the group recovers w, because the sink is the output and the input is w(output) plus zero. Every tuple of local instruments therefore induces a normalized probability, and the Choi operator of U is a valid unitary process.

Unitarity of the local operators does not kill the shift by itself. The process-function clause does. That is why the swap and the identity loop fail the same contraction: they fail step 1, so the off-diagonal need not vanish. This identification with complex local unitaries was not kernel-checked. Row orthogonality is the standard step. The step that could have failed is the combinatorial one, and it did not.

8. What the sketch does and does not close

If step 3 stands, every classical deterministic process function purifies to a unitary process, and the dilation is the source-and-sink permutation itself. The 2019 question is then closed. The 2026 conjecture of Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal is a theorem, and their sufficient conditions, mutual exclusivity of control conditions and unambiguity, are not the reason. Lugano sits outside that subclass and was already known to work by the 2017 construction of Araujo, Feix, Navascues and Brukner, rewritten as a routed circuit by Vanrietvelde, Ormrod, Kristjansson and Barrett. Those are existence proofs for one function. The sketch is a universal quantifier, conditional on the contraction.

If step 3 does not stand, the note has proved the lemma and the bit bijection, which the argument uses, and has not closed the conjecture. The paper claims step 3. It does not claim that the kernel accepted it.

9. Embeddability, at the length the question deserves

Three things have been called an embedding, and only one is the gap.

A post-selected circuit can make the Lugano event list appear in the runs that survive. That is a simulation. The probability of the post-selection is not one. The inconsistent branch is thrown away rather than never having been a solution.

The source-and-sink bijection embeds the function in a larger function. That is reversibility. The 2019 paper realises the extension by interactions among bounded regions. It does not exhibit a metric. The extra coordinates are operational boundaries. Lean checked that they restore a bijection on bits. A throat would be a junction, with an energy condition. None of that is in the certificate.

A Lorentzian embedding would be a local flow on a fixed metric whose solutions, for every local intervention, are exactly the fixed points of the process function. Only this is the gap.

The ordered case closes. The twelve binary two-party process functions are channels. A channel embeds in Minkowski spacetime as free propagation from the earlier party to the later one, with the local instrument at the later end. No wormhole is required. Baumeler and Wolf already proved that a classical violation of a causal inequality needs three parties, so every two-party process function is of this kind.

The cyclic case does not close by the same route. Lugano violates a causal inequality, so no wiring of the three parties in a definite order, and no mixture of orders, reproduces it if the settings are free. A fixed metric with a definite causal order cannot embed it. A metric with closed timelike curves can, in principle. What is missing is a local Hamiltonian on such a metric whose consistency equation is the process-function equation and whose multiplicity is one for every intervention. The billiard is a local Hamiltonian and has the wrong multiplicity. No other one is known. Adding a source and a sink does not supply the metric. It supplies two extra regions. Dourdent and collaborators leave the physical side open on purpose. This note does the same.

Lugano is two-to-one on its original alphabet. The input-output relation is not the graph of a reversible Hamiltonian flow on those three bits. The extension restores reversibility by enlarging the alphabet. That is an argument that the original three bits are the wrong phase space, not an argument that a phase space has been exhibited.

10. Adjacent arguments, not answered

Price argued that a time-symmetric ontology for quantum theory should be retrocausal. Leifer and Pusey replaced reality of the quantum state with lambda-mediation and obtained a timelike Bell factorization, which sequential measurements violate. Maudlin replied that the operational time-reverse of an experiment is not always an experiment of the same kind. The dilation is adjacent to that argument, not a solution of it. A process function that violates a causal inequality already has each input depending on another output. Past-screening mediation fails because of the cycle. A cycle is what the no-go says a time-symmetric ontology must allow, if the other assumptions stand. Exhibiting a cycle does not answer the no-go.

Freedom of choice is the assumption that the intervention is not itself a function of the output. The process-function clause assumes it, in the form of a quantification over all local functions. It does not derive it. Unique completion is not openness of the choice. Purifiability of every extensibly causal process remains open. The sketch covers deterministic classical process functions only.

11. Ledger

Shown: the disagreement lemma, any finite alphabet. Shown: the bit bijection, both compositions. Shown: Lugano by enumeration, and the failure of the identity and the cycle. Sketched, not kernel-checked: the complex contraction, and the instrument step by Stinespring. Not claimed: a metric for Lugano, an answer to Leifer and Pusey, a settlement of freedom.
