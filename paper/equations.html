Unique completion, the unitary lift, and what is not a metric

Equations are set in the page, with no external script. The claims are not run together.

1. Register

Claim A is checked. On bits, a process function cannot send two distinct output strings that agree in the function wherever they differ, and the source-and-sink extension is a bijection. Lean 4.34.1 accepted both in lean/Full.lean.

Claim B is written. That witness is the cancellation in the source-to-sink gram matrix. Granted row orthonormality, the induced map is unitary. Instruments follow by Stinespring, which is cited and not formalised.

Claim C is a split. Ordered process functions embed as channels. The cyclic case has a post-selected simulation and a reversible extension, and no known local Hamiltonian on a wormhole whose solutions are exactly its fixed points.

2. The demand

Dummett set the dictionary objection aside in 1954 and again in 1964. A stipulation about the word cause is not a discovery. Faye's Stanford entry, revised 28 October 2025, still treats the temporal order as contingent and the consistency paradoxes as unresolved. Black's bilking argument is the objection that survives. Lewis splits the modal and does not select the histories. Horwich asks a theory to say what happens to the intervention.

A process function answers by a restriction on the admitted maps. An intervention is a tuple of local functions. The map is admitted only when the composition has one fixed point. No fixed point is the grandfather case. Two is the bootstrap. The clause can be failed from inside the theory.

3. The objects

A process function w sends an output string to an input string. A local intervention f maps each party's input to that party's output. The fixed-point equation is

f(w(o)) = o.

The map is a process function when every f has exactly one solution o.

The Lugano function is

w(a, b, c) = (¬b ∧ c, ¬c ∧ a, ¬a ∧ b).

Each input depends on the other two outputs. All sixty-four binary interventions have one fixed point. The image has four elements out of eight, so w is two-to-one.

The source-and-sink extension is the bijection

U |o, e⟩ = |w(o) ⊕ e, o⟩,

with inverse

U−1 |i, s⟩ = |s, i ⊕ w(s)⟩.

Both compositions are the identity on bits. Lean checked them. A finite bijection is a permutation unitary on the computational basis. That is representation, not yet a quantum process.

4. Sketch of the proof

Lemma. If w is a process function and s ≠ s', then some party k has sk ≠ s'k and wk(s) = wk(s').

Otherwise define

fk(x) = sk if x = wk(s),   s'k if x = wk(s'),   0 otherwise.

Then f(w(s)) = s and f(w(s')) = s'. One intervention has two fixed points. The swap fails the other way: under w(a, b) = (b, a), negation on each side fixes both (0, 1) and (1, 0). Lean accepted this as process_disagreement.

The source-to-sink amplitude, with a local unitary Vk at each party, is

Ms,e = ∏k ⟨sk| Vk |wk(s) ⊕ ek⟩.

The gram entry is the sum over e of Ms,e times the conjugate of Ms',e. Change variables by ik = wk(s) ⊕ ek. The shift is

δk = wk(s') ⊕ wk(s).

The factored gram entry is

(MM†)s,s' = ∏k ∑i ⟨sk|Vk|i⟩ ⟨i ⊕ δk| Vk† |s'k⟩.

If s = s', every shift vanishes and each factor is a row norm, hence 1. If s ≠ s', the lemma supplies a party with δk = 0 and sk ≠ s'k. That factor is the inner product of two distinct rows, and vanishes. So

MM† = I.

A local instrument is a unitary on an ancilla followed by a partial trace. Fixing the source at 0 recovers w. The kernel did not check this sum, nor row orthonormality over the complex numbers. The integer rows (1, 1) and (1, −1) have dot product 0. A normalised Hadamard needs √2 and is not in the file.

5. What would be new

Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych prove the reversible extension and say the quantum step is open. Araújo, Feix, Navascués and Brukner quantize Lugano. Vanrietvelde, Ormrod, Kristjánsson and Barrett rewrite that extension as a routed circuit. Those are one function. Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal conjecture the general lift and prove it under two sufficient conditions, neither necessary. The sketch does not use those conditions. The forbidden double fixed point is the forbidden off-diagonal. That identification is written, not kernel-checked.

6. Spacetime, and the split

The billiard of Echeverria, Klinkhammer and Thorne has the wrong multiplicity. A channel embeds without a wormhole:

iB = w(oA),   iA independent of oB,

or the reverse. Lugano has no such wiring if the settings are free. Dourdent and collaborators leave the physical side open. The absence of a known Hamiltonian is not a proof that none exists.

7. Adjacent, not answered

Price, Leifer and Pusey, and Maudlin are adjacent. A cycle drops an operational screening condition. It does not choose an ontology. Unique completion is not an open choice.
