Unique completion, the unitary lift, and what is not a metric

About half again as long as the previous note. Equations are displayed. The claims are not run together.

1. Register

Claim A is checked. On bits, a process function cannot send two distinct output strings that agree in the function wherever they differ, and the source-and-sink extension is a bijection. Lean 4.34.1 accepted both in lean/Full.lean.

Claim B is written. That witness is the cancellation in the source-to-sink gram matrix. Granted row orthonormality, the induced map is unitary. Instruments follow by Stinespring, which is cited and not formalised.

Claim C is a split. Ordered process functions embed as channels. The cyclic case has a post-selected simulation and a reversible extension, and no known local Hamiltonian on a wormhole whose solutions are exactly its fixed points.

2. The demand

Dummett set the dictionary objection aside in 1954 and again in 1964. A stipulation about the word cause is not a discovery. Faye's Stanford entry, revised 28 October 2025, still treats the temporal order as contingent and the consistency paradoxes as unresolved. Black's bilking argument is the objection that survives: an agent who has seen the earlier event can try to prevent the later one. Lewis splits the modal and does not select the histories. Horwich asks a theory to say what happens to the intervention.

A process function answers by a restriction on the admitted maps. An intervention is a tuple of local functions. The map is admitted only when the composition has one fixed point. No fixed point is the grandfather case. Two is the bootstrap. The clause can be failed from inside the theory. The identity loop fails it. The swap fails it. A rider that assigns inconsistent solutions measure zero cannot be failed in that way.

3. The objects

Parties have finite alphabets. On bits the group law is exclusive or. A process function $w$ sends an output string to an input string. A local intervention $f$ is a tuple of maps from a party's input to that party's output. The fixed-point equation is

$$ f\bigl(w(o)\bigr) = o. $$

The map is a process function when every $f$ has exactly one solution $o$.

The Lugano function, found by Araújo and Feix, is

$$ w(a,b,c) = (\lnot b \land c,\ \lnot c \land a,\ \lnot a \land b). $$

Each input depends on the other two outputs. All sixty-four binary interventions have one fixed point. The image has four elements out of eight, so $w$ is two-to-one. Reversibility is not free on the original alphabet.

The source-and-sink extension of Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych is the bijection

$$ U\lvert o, e\rangle = \lvert w(o)\oplus e,\ o\rangle, $$

with inverse

$$ U^{-1}\lvert i, s\rangle = \lvert s,\ i\oplus w(s)\rangle. $$

Both compositions are the identity for every $w$, process function or not. Lean checked them on bits. A bijection of a finite set is a permutation unitary on the computational basis. That is representation, not yet a quantum process.

4. Sketch of the proof

Lemma. If $w$ is a process function and $s\neq s'$, then some party $k$ has $s_k\neq s'_k$ and $w_k(s)=w_k(s')$.

Suppose not. Agreement of the function values already forces agreement of the strings. Define

$$ f_k(x) = \begin{cases} s_k & \text{if } x = w_k(s),\\ s'_k & \text{if } x = w_k(s'),\\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases} $$

The two clauses agree wherever the inputs agree, so $f_k$ is a function. Then $f(w(s))=s$ and $f(w(s'))=s'$. One intervention has two fixed points. A process function cannot have that. The swap is sharp the other way: under $w(a,b)=(b,a)$, negation on each side fixes both $(0,1)$ and $(1,0)$.

This is the step the kernel accepted, as process_disagreement. Free choice does the logical work. The agent at party $k$ may apply any local function, including the one just defined.

For the contraction, put a local unitary $V_k$ at each party, possibly on a local ancilla. The source-to-sink amplitude is

$$ M_{s,e} = \prod_k \langle s_k\rvert V_k \lvert w_k(s)\oplus e_k\rangle. $$

The gram entry is $\sum_e M_{s,e} M_{s',e}^{*}$. Change variables by $i_k = w_k(s)\oplus e_k$. Exclusive or with a fixed string is a bijection, so the sum is a product of local overlaps against the shift

$$ \delta_k = w_k(s')\oplus w_k(s). $$

Explicitly,

$$ (MM^{\dagger})_{s,s'} = \prod_k \sum_{i_k} \langle s_k\rvert V_k\lvert i_k\rangle\, \langle i_k\oplus\delta_k\rvert V_k^{\dagger}\lvert s'_k\rangle. $$

If $s=s'$, every shift vanishes and each factor is a row norm, hence $1$. If $s\neq s'$, the lemma supplies a party with $\delta_k=0$ and $s_k\neq s'_k$. That factor is the inner product of two distinct rows of a unitary, and vanishes. One zero factor kills the product, so $MM^{\dagger}=I$. The induced map is unitary.

A local instrument is a unitary on an ancilla followed by a partial trace. The partial trace of a unitary dilation is completely positive and trace-preserving. Fixing the source at $0$ recovers $w$, because the input is then $w(o)$ and the sink records $o$. The purification is not a second consistency axiom.

The kernel did not check the identification of the gram entry with this sum, nor row orthonormality as a theorem about $\mathbb{C}$. The integer rows $(1,1)$ and $(1,-1)$ have dot product $0$. A normalised Hadamard needs $\sqrt{2}$ and is not in the file.

5. What would be new

Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych prove the reversible extension and say the quantum step is open. They check one finite-dimensional case. Araújo, Feix, Navascués and Brukner quantize Lugano. Vanrietvelde, Ormrod, Kristjánsson and Barrett rewrite that extension as a routed circuit. Those are one function. Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal conjecture the general lift and prove it under mutual exclusivity of control conditions and under unambiguity, noting that neither is necessary. The sketch does not use those conditions. The forbidden double fixed point is the forbidden off-diagonal. That identification is the novel step, and it is written.

6. Spacetime, and the split

Gödel's solution has closed timelike curves. Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever convert a traversable wormhole into a time machine. Friedman and collaborators keep only self-consistent solutions. Echeverria, Klinkhammer and Thorne find billiard data with one continuation, two, or none. Carlini, Frolov, Mensky, Novikov and Solodukhin recover the same underdetermination from the action. Earman's warning is that a condition imposed after the dynamics is not a dynamics. Hawking's chronology protection conjectures that the curve does not form. That is a different question.

Deutsch asks for a fixed point of the partial trace. Existence is a theorem. Uniqueness is not. Aaronson and Watrous place the power at PSPACE. Bennett, Leung, Smith and Smolin call the power an artefact. Tolksdorf and Verch show that the condition can be met on a spacetime with no closed curve. Lloyd and collaborators replace the fixed point by post-selection. Process functions sit in that class. The event list is conditional on success. That is a simulation.

The process matrix of Oreshkov, Costa and Brukner gives a probability for every instrument. The quantum switch of Chiribella, D'Ariano, Perinotti and Valiron does not violate a causal inequality. Lugano does. Baumeler and Wolf proved that a classical violation needs three parties, so every two-party process function is a channel and embeds as free propagation. A channel is

$$ i_B = w(o_A),\qquad i_A\ \text{independent of } o_B, $$

or the reverse. No wormhole is required. Lugano has no such wiring if the settings are free. Dourdent, Leitherer, Boghiu, Simonov, Kunjwal and Acín leave the physical side open. The pairwise-exclusive event list does not say which lists are fixed points of a Hamiltonian flow. The billiard has the wrong multiplicity. No other local flow is known. The absence is not a proof that none exists.

7. Adjacent, not answered

Price argued that a time-symmetric ontology should be retrocausal. Leifer and Pusey, under lambda-mediation, obtain a timelike Bell factorization, which sequential measurements violate. Maudlin replies that the operational reverse of an experiment need not be an experiment of the same kind. A cyclic dependence drops an operational screening condition. It does not choose an ontology. Specker's pairwise exclusivity is the shape of the event list, not a derivation of freedom. Unique completion is not an open choice.
