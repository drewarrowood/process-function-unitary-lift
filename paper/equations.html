Unique completion, the unitary lift, and what is not a metric

The displays are the paper's. On bits, plus and minus are exclusive or. No external script.

1. Register

Claim A is checked. On bits, a process function cannot send two distinct output strings that agree in the function wherever they differ, and the source-and-sink extension is a bijection. Lean 4.34.1 accepted both in lean/Full.lean.

Claim B is written. That witness is the cancellation in the source-to-sink gram matrix. Instruments follow by Stinespring, cited and not formalised.

Claim C is a split. Ordered process functions embed as channels. The cyclic case has a simulation and a reversible extension, and no known local Hamiltonian on a wormhole whose solutions are exactly its fixed points.

2. The objects

The fixed point is in the output:

o = f(w(o)). (1)

The Lugano function is

w(a, b, c) = (¬b ∧ c, ¬c ∧ a, ¬a ∧ b). (2)

The source-and-sink unitary is

U |o, e⟩ = |w(o) + e, o⟩, (3)

with inverse

U−1 |i, s⟩ = |s, i − w(s)⟩. (4)

On bits, plus in (3) and minus in (4) are exclusive or, which is what Lean checked. A finite bijection is a permutation unitary on the computational basis. That is representation, not yet a quantum process.

3. Sketch of the proof

Lemma. If w is a process function and s ≠ s′, some party k has

sk ≠ s′k and wk(s) = wk(s′). (5)

Otherwise both strings solve (1) for one intervention. Lean accepted (5) as process_disagreement.

The source-to-sink amplitude is

Ms,e = ∏k ⟨sk| Vk |wk(s) + ek⟩. (6)

Change variables by ik = wk(s) + ek. The shift is

δk = wk(s′) − wk(s). (7)

The gram entry is

(MM†)s,s′ = ∏k ⟨row sk | row s′k shifted by δk⟩. (8)

If s = s′, every shift vanishes and each factor is 1. If s ≠ s′, (5) supplies a party with δk = 0 and distinct rows, so that factor is 0. Therefore

MM† = I. (9)

(9) is written, not kernel-checked. Fixing the source at 0 in (3) recovers w. A normalised Hadamard needs the square root of 2 and is not in the file.

4. What is not a metric

A channel is

iB = w(oA), iA independent of oB, (10)

or the reverse. (10) embeds as free propagation. (2) violates a causal inequality, so no wiring of that form embeds it. (3) supplies two extra regions, not a metric.
