Every process function lifts, and the embeddability gap

A process function is a map from outputs to inputs with exactly one fixed point for every local intervention. No fixed point is the grandfather case. More than one is the bootstrap. Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych extend every such map to a bijection by a source and a sink, and leave the quantum step open. The written argument is that the process-function clause is already the cancellation. The kernel checks the combinatorial half and the bijection. A spacetime embedding is a separate question, and the attempt to close it splits the class in two.

1. Bilking, and what a criterion has to do

Dummett set aside the claim that a cause is earlier by definition. Faye's Stanford entry, revised 28 October 2025, still treats the order of cause and effect as contingent, and still treats the consistency paradoxes as open. Black's bilking argument is the objection that survives: an agent who has seen the earlier event can try to prevent the later one. Lewis splits the modal. Locally the attempt is possible. Globally it is not, because the past already contains the failure. That names the tension. It does not generate the failure. A process function generates it as non-admission. The attempt is a local function. If the composition has no fixed point, the pair is not a process.

2. Spacetime, and three quantum replies

Gödel's solution has closed timelike curves. Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever turn a traversable wormhole into a time machine if the throat is held open by exotic stress-energy. Friedman and collaborators keep only self-consistent solutions. Echeverria, Klinkhammer and Thorne find billiard data with one continuation, two, or none. Earman's warning still holds. A condition imposed after the dynamics is not a dynamics.

Deutsch asks for a fixed point of the partial trace. Existence is a theorem in finite dimension. Uniqueness is not. Post-selection, as in Lloyd and collaborators, keeps the inconsistent branch in the circuit and out of the sample. The process matrix of Oreshkov, Costa and Brukner asks for a probability for every instrument, with no global order assumed. Process functions are the deterministic classical points of that class.

3. The lemma, the bijection, and what was checked

process_disagreement, in lean/Full.lean, Lean 4.34.1, no unfinished proof. If w is a process function on bits and s differs from s', some party disagrees in the output and agrees in w. Otherwise one local function fixes both.

extend_left_inv and extend_right_inv. The map (o, e) ↦ (w(o) XOR e, o) is a bijection. The inverse is written down.

The written cancellation uses that witness. Change variables by ik = wk(s) XOR ek. The gram matrix factors into row overlaps against the shift. Off the diagonal the witness party contributes the inner product of two distinct rows. If the rows are orthonormal, the induced source-to-sink map is unitary. Instruments follow by a partial trace. That last paragraph is not in the kernel. The kernel has the witness and the bijection. It does not have the sum, a general complex unitary, or Stinespring.

4. Novelty

Araújo, Feix, Navascués and Brukner quantized Lugano in 2017. The 2019 paper left the general quantum step open. Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal conjectured the general lift on 30 September 2026 and proved it under two sufficient conditions, mutual exclusivity of control conditions and unambiguity. This argument does not use those conditions. The new step is the identification of the forbidden double fixed point with the off-diagonal. The identification is written. The prohibition on the double fixed point is checked.

5. The embeddability gap

The gap is physical, not algebraic. A process function is a list of events. A spacetime is a metric. Nothing in the cancellation turns one into the other. Three different things have been called an embedding.

A post-selected circuit can make the Lugano event list appear in the runs that survive. That is a simulation. Process functions sit inside post-selected closed timelike curves. The event list is conditional on the post-selection succeeding. It is not an account of why the inconsistent branch was never a physical option.

The source-and-sink bijection embeds the function in a larger function. That is reversibility. The 2019 paper realises it by interactions among bounded regions. It does not exhibit a metric. Adding a source and a sink supplies two extra regions, not a throat. Lugano is two-to-one on its original alphabet, so the input-output relation is not the graph of a reversible Hamiltonian flow on those three bits. Lean checked the restored bijection. The extra coordinates are operational boundaries.

A Lorentzian embedding would be a local flow on a fixed metric whose solutions, for every local intervention, are exactly the fixed points of the process function. Only this third reading is the gap Dourdent, Leitherer, Boghiu, Simonov, Kunjwal and Acín left open. Their event list is complete and pairwise exclusive. They do not say which of those lists arise as fixed points of a dynamical feedback.

6. Why the billiard does not close it

Echeverria, Klinkhammer and Thorne integrated the hard-sphere billiard on the Morris–Thorne wormhole and found initial data with one self-consistent continuation, data with two, and data with none apparent. A process function demands exactly one fixed point for every intervention. The billiard has the wrong multiplicity, so the Lugano list is not the fixed-point set of that flow. Deutsch's model keeps the multiplicity and averages it. Post-selection throws the extra solutions away. Neither is an embedding.

The obstruction is the quantifier. Any ordinary local operation remains allowed. A Hamiltonian tuned so that one interaction has a unique continuation does not meet that quantifier. The billiard is a local Hamiltonian and fails it on an open set of data. No other local Hamiltonian on a wormhole metric is known to enforce uniqueness for every local operation.

7. Where the gap splits

The ordered case closes. The twelve binary two-party process functions are channels. Four have no signalling, four signal one way, four the other. A channel embeds in Minkowski spacetime as free propagation from the earlier party to the later one, with the local instrument at the later end. No wormhole is required. Baumeler and Wolf proved that a classical violation of a causal inequality needs three parties, so every two-party process function is of this kind.

The cyclic case does not close by the same route. Lugano violates a causal inequality, so no wiring of the three parties in a definite order, and no mixture of orders, reproduces it if the settings are free. A fixed metric with a definite causal order cannot embed it. A metric with closed timelike curves can, in principle. What is missing is a local Hamiltonian on such a metric whose consistency equation is the process-function equation and whose multiplicity is one for every intervention. The purification does not supply that Hamiltonian. It is a third object, beside the simulation and the embedding.

The attempt to close the gap is this split. Ordered process functions embed as channels. Cyclic ones have a reversible extension, checked as a bijection, and a written unitary purification, and no known local flow on a wormhole whose solutions are exactly their fixed points. A delay line in ordinary time can realise the list as a circuit with a constraint. That is an embedding in a wiring diagram, not in a spacetime. The cyclic case stays open.

8. What is not claimed

Not a decision about freedom. Unique completion is not an open choice. Not a solution of the Leifer–Pusey no-go. Not a refereed theorem. Unique completion answers the logical half of the demand in Faye's entry. It does not answer the geometric half.
