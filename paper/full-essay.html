Every process function lifts to a unitary process

Open extension is a bijection for every w. Closed contraction is not an isometry for every w: identity loop, gram diagonal 2^n. Conjugation is a compiled involution. No Einstein metric for Lugano. Stories kept. References numbered.

The clerk and the closed window

Mara cannot hand in the form that would prevent the telegram. No fixed point. The story cannot be finished.

The note in the coat

Two wordings, two fixed points. The bootstrap is a failure of uniqueness. The line that differs and the line that agrees are the witness.

Open case

\(U(o,e)=(w(o)\oplus e,\, o)\) and \(U^{-1}(i,s)=(s,\, w(s)\oplus i)\). Both compositions are the identity by \(a\oplus(a\oplus b)=b\), for every \(w\).

Closed case

Against local identities, \(J_{s,e}=\prod_k 1[s_k=w_k(s)\oplus e_k]\). For \(w(s)=s\) and \(e=0\), \((J^\dagger J)_{0,0}=2^n\). Three parties: 8, not 1.
