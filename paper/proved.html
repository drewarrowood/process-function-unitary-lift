What has been proved

Every classical process function on bits extends to a bijection by a source and a sink, and no process function can have two distinct output strings that agree in the function wherever they differ. That is the kernel-checked part. The identification of that witness with the vanishing of a complex gram matrix, and the passage from that unitary to a completely positive instrument, are written proofs. They are not in the kernel.

1. The claim, split

A process function is a map from output strings to input strings such that every local deterministic intervention has exactly one fixed point. Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych proved in 2019 that every such map extends to a reversible process by a source and a sink, and left the quantum step open. Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal conjectured on 30 September 2026 that the extension is a valid unitary process, and proved that conjecture under two sufficient conditions this argument does not use. The written theorem is that the process-function clause is already the cancellation. The file in the repository checks the combinatorial half and the bijection, not the complex half.

2. What the kernel accepted

The file is lean/Full.lean. The note beside it, lean/FULL-NOTE.md, records Lean 4.34.1, no Mathlib, no unfinished proof, exit status 0. Three theorems are in that file.

process_disagreement. For every number of parties, on bits: if w is a process function and s differs from s', some party disagrees in the output bit and agrees in the value of w. The proof builds the local intervention that would otherwise fix both strings. Uniqueness forbids it.

extend_left_inv and extend_right_inv. The map (o, e) ↦ (w(o) XOR e, o) is a bijection. The inverse is written down. On the computational basis that bijection is a permutation unitary.

hadamard_rows_orthogonal. The integer rows (1, 1) and (1, −1) have dot product 0. This is a witness that a Hadamard-shaped pair of rows is orthogonal over the integers. It is not the contraction of a complex Hadamard, which needs √2.

lean/Cancel.lean is an earlier certificate. It checks, by finite evaluation, that the Lugano function is a process function, that it satisfies the witness property, and that the identity and the cycle do not. That count is not the general lemma. The general lemma is process_disagreement.

3. What is written, and not checked

The gram entry is the sum over the source of the product, over parties, of the amplitude for the local unitary to take the shifted source bit to the sink bit. Change variables by ik = wk(s) XOR ek. The sum factors. On the diagonal every shift vanishes and each factor is a row norm. Off the diagonal process_disagreement supplies a party whose shift is zero and whose outputs differ, so that factor is the inner product of two distinct rows. If those rows are orthonormal, the factor is zero and the gram matrix is the identity.

That last sentence is the written step. The kernel has the witness party and a bijection. It does not have the sum, the change of variables as a theorem about the contracted operator, or row orthonormality of a general complex unitary. A local instrument is a unitary on a local ancilla followed by a partial trace. That is Stinespring, and it is not formalised. Alphabets other than bits are not formalised.

4. What is new, if the written step stands

The novelty is the universal quantifier, not a new construction of Lugano. Araújo, Feix, Navascués and Brukner quantized Lugano in 2017. Vanrietvelde, Ormrod, Kristjánsson and Barrett wrote that extension as a routed circuit. Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych left the general quantum step open. Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal conjectured it and proved it under mutual exclusivity of control conditions and under unambiguity. The argument here does not use those conditions. The new step is the identification of the forbidden double fixed point with the off-diagonal the contraction kills. That identification is written. The half of it that says a double fixed point is forbidden is checked.

5. What is not claimed

Not a spacetime embedding. The wormhole billiard has the wrong multiplicity. Not a decision about freedom. Unique completion is not an open choice. Not a solution of the Leifer–Pusey no-go. Not a refereed theorem. Faye's Stanford entry, revised 28 October 2025, still treats the consistency paradoxes as unresolved. The checked half answers one point of that entry: unique completion already forces a witness party, and the reversible extension is a bijection. It does not yet answer the entry with a kernel-checked unitary process.
