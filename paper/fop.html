Every process function lifts to a unitary process

Draft in the manner of Foundations of Physics. Not a submission. Shown, claimed, and not claimed are marked in the text.

Abstract

A process function is a classical map from the outputs of several parties to their inputs such that every local deterministic intervention has exactly one fixed point. Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych proved that every such map extends to a bijection by a source and a sink, and left open whether the bijection quantizes. Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal conjectured that it does. This paper separates what is shown from what is claimed. The disagreement lemma is shown for any finite alphabet. The source-and-sink map is shown to be a bijection on bits. The unitary contraction against complex local instruments is claimed in writing and is not kernel-checked. Embeddability of the cyclic case in a spacetime is not claimed. Ordered process functions embed as channels. The Lugano function does not, on any metric that is known.

1. What is shown

Three statements are theorems in a kernel. If a process function on a finite alphabet sends two distinct output strings, some party disagrees in the output symbol and agrees in the function value. On bits, the source-and-sink extension is a bijection, and the inverse is the same form with the roles exchanged. The Lugano function has one fixed point for every binary intervention, and the identity loop and the three-cycle do not. These are Lean 4.34.1 certificates, with no unfinished proof.

One statement is a written argument. The permutation unitary of the extension induces a unitary map from source to sink against any tuple of local unitaries, ancilla included, because the gram matrix factors and the lemma kills the off-diagonal by row orthogonality. Instruments follow by Stinespring. This is the step that would close the 2019 question and the 2026 conjecture. It is not in the kernel.

One statement is refused. Nothing in the cancellation is a metric. Ordered process functions embed as channels in Minkowski spacetime. The cyclic case has a simulation by post-selection, a reversible extension, and no known local Hamiltonian whose solutions are exactly its fixed points.

2. Four demands

Backward causation is asked to be consistent, to be falsifiable, to be reversible, and to be quantum. Consistency is the empty case and the overfull case. Novikov's principle refuses the first after the fact and leaves the second as a multiplicity. A uniqueness clause blocks both, and it can be failed. Faye's Stanford entry, revised 28 October 2025, still treats that as unresolved. The process-function clause is a finite-alphabet answer, and only that.

Reversibility is the demand that a time-symmetric law not be given a one-way reading. Lugano is two-to-one. The source and the sink restore a bijection. The extra coordinates are a boundary. The quantum demand is that instruments have a joint probability. The written contraction is the claim that the clause which blocked the double fixed point also kills the off-diagonal.

3. Bilking

Dummett treated the dictionary objection as a stipulation. Black's bilking argument is the serious one. Lewis splits the modal and does not select the histories. The process-function clause selects them. An intervention is a local function. The global map is admitted only if that function has one fixed point. Bilking is not forbidden as an action. It is constrained as a solution. That is the no-new-physics principle.

4. The billiard

Godel 1949, Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever 1988, Friedman and collaborators 1990, Echeverria, Klinkhammer and Thorne 1991. The billiard has one continuation, two, and none apparent. Earman's warning stands. The billiard is a local Hamiltonian and does not have one solution for every boundary condition. It is not an embedding of Lugano. Post-selection simulates the event list. The source and the sink make the map reversible. A metric would be a third object. None of the three is the other.

5. Quantum replies

Deutsch's fixed point exists and is nonlinear. Aaronson and Watrous put the power at PSPACE. Bennett and collaborators called it an artefact. Tolksdorf and Verch showed the condition can be met with no closed curve. Lloyd and collaborators post-select. Oreshkov, Costa and Brukner define a process with no global order. The switch is causally nonseparable and does not violate a causal inequality. Lugano violates one. A unitary process here is the Choi operator of the source-and-sink unitary. It is operational. It is not a solution of Einstein's equation.

6. The lemma and the bijection

If two output strings differ and no party is a witness, one local function fixes both. Lean accepted this for bits and for a general finite alphabet. On bits the source-and-sink map is a bijection; exclusive or cancels; Lean accepted both compositions. A bijection is a permutation unitary on the computational basis. That is all the kernel says about unitarity.

7. The contraction, claimed and not checked

The source-to-sink amplitude is a product of local amplitudes. The gram entry factors. On the diagonal the shift vanishes. Off it the lemma supplies a party whose rows differ and whose shift vanishes, so that factor is zero. Instruments follow by Stinespring. This identification with complex local unitaries was not kernel-checked. The paper claims it. It does not claim that the kernel accepted it.

8. Novelty, conditional

Araujo and collaborators quantized Lugano in 2017. That is one function. The 2019 paper left the general step open. Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal conjectured the general lift and proved it under two sufficient conditions that are not necessary. The novelty, if the contraction stands, is that the process-function clause is the cancellation. If it does not stand, the paper has the lemma and the bijection, and has not closed the conjecture.

9. Embeddability

Ordered process functions embed as channels. The cyclic case does not. Lugano violates a causal inequality, so no mixture of definite orders reproduces it if the settings are free. The billiard has the wrong multiplicity. No other local Hamiltonian is known. A source and a sink are regions, not mouths. Dourdent and collaborators leave the physical side open. This paper does the same.

10. Not claimed

Freedom is not settled. The Leifer-Pusey no-go is not answered. Purifiability of every extensibly causal process remains open. The written claim covers deterministic classical process functions only.

Ledger

Shown: disagreement lemma, any finite alphabet. Shown: bit bijection. Shown: Lugano by enumeration. Claimed, not kernel-checked: complex contraction and instruments. Not claimed: a metric for Lugano.
