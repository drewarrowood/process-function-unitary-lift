# Audit: European Journal for Philosophy of Science draft

Journal: European Journal for Philosophy of Science, Springer, ISSN 1879-4912 / 1879-4920. Official journal of the European Philosophy of Science Association.

Page retrieved: https://link.springer.com/journal/13194/submission-guidelines

From that page: double-blind review; anonymize the manuscript; separate title page with name, affiliation, contact, acknowledgements, disclosures, and funding. Original research papers approximately 5,000 to 15,000 words. Abstract 150 to 250 words, no undefined abbreviations or unspecified references. Four to six keywords. Author-year citations; reference list alphabetized; DOIs as full links when available. Footnotes rather than endnotes, not citation-only. Decimal headings, at most three levels. Statements and declarations are required for a published paper and are placed on the title page for review. Large language models are not authors. Generative use beyond copy editing is documented in the manuscript (there is no methods section, so a short section is used) and on the title page. The Springer Nature LaTeX template is recommended and was not used; the draft is 11 pt TeX Gyre Termes. The guide's Word example is 10 pt Times. That formatting difference is a deviation, not claimed as template compliance. A third-party template site was not used as policy.

Counts: abstract 198 words; four keywords; pre-bibliography source count about 5,000 words; PDF about 5,200 words and 9 pages. Inside the approximate 5,000–15,000 range. The review PDF does not contain the author's name.

Primary texts read for this argument: Karaca, BJPS 73 (2022) 279–298; Kuorikoski, Lehtinen, and Marchionni, PhilSci-Archive preprint, 25 September 2007. Orzack and Sober, Sugden, and Woodward are present only as those texts report them. Hausman and Woodward 1999 is cited for a contrast already used in another adaptation and is not re-derived here.

The piece is an alternative adaptation of the public finite results. It asks what an intervention test would have to identify. It does not claim those identifications have been carried out. Affiliation, email, ORCID, city, country, funding, and competing interests are pending and are not filled in as "none." Human verification is pending. The model version is unrecorded. No submission was made. Anonymity is not claimed.
