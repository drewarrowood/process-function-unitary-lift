# Audit: Physical Review A draft

Journal: Physical Review A (APS). Regular Article. Letters are a different type with a 4500-word limit; this draft is not a Letter.

Pages used:
- https://journals.aps.org/pra/authors (article types: Regular Articles have no length limit; single-anonymized review; data-availability statement required; AI disclosure required for substantive use)
- https://journals.aps.org/authors/style-basics (abstract: one self-contained paragraph, no displayed equations, about 5% of the article and under 500 words; numbered references are not used inside the abstract)
- https://journals.aps.org/authors/length-guide (word-count formula for letters and short papers; not applied as a cap here because the type is a Regular Article)
- https://journals.aps.org/authors/appropriate-use-ai-tools (substantive drafting disclosed in the acknowledgments; tool name, how it assisted, how it was directed; version is not recorded and is not invented; AI is not an author)
- https://journals.aps.org/authors/data-availability-statements (statement in the manuscript; tables are the data; repository citation has no DOI)

Format: REVTeX 4.2 with options aps, pra, reprint. The class was not installed in TeX Live. The runtime files under paper/revtex/ were unpacked from the LPPL dtx sources (REVTeX 4.2f). References use apsrev4-2 and include titles, which PRA requests.

Counts: abstract 158 words. PDF text about 2700 words, 4 pages in reprint (two-column) format. The abstract is under 500 words and near 5% of the article.

What is proved in the file: the canonical equivalence (Theorem 1), including matched finite ancillas, and the numerical value of L on the known board by exact contraction of the printed tables. What is not proved in the file: validity of p under every local stochastic map, vanishing of the 2232 cyclotomic coefficients, and L greater than or equal to 0 on the 744 three-bit process functions. Those remain external finite checks in the public repository. The board is the known Guérin–Brukner-family witness, not a new device. No speedup is claimed. Baire-class-one fixed points are stated as open and unused.

The mathematics is the same as in the public technical note and the Quantum-style draft. This is an alternative adaptation, not an independent study. Affiliation, email, ORCID, funding, and conflicts are pending. Human verification is pending. No submission was made.
