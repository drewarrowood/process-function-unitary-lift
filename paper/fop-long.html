Unique completion, a sketch of the lift, and the embeddability gap

About twice the previous essay. The proof is a sketch, and the sketch says which steps were checked.

Claims register. Shown: if a map has one fixed point for every local intervention, distinct output strings leave a party who disagrees in the output and agrees in the function value. On bits, the source-and-sink extension is a bijection. Both are in lean/Full.lean. Written: that witness kills the off-diagonal of the gram matrix of the permutation unitary, so the induced source-to-sink map is unitary, and instruments follow by Stinespring. Not claimed: a metric, a wormhole Hamiltonian for Lugano, a solution of the Leifer-Pusey no-go, freedom, or a refereed theorem.

1. The demand

Faye's Stanford entry, revised 28 October 2025, still treats the consistency paradoxes as unresolved. A logic that allows the effect to precede the cause has to block bootstrap loops and inconsistency loops without collapsing into the stipulation that only consistent loops occur. This essay answers one reading of that demand. Unique completion is a property of the admitted maps. It is not a filter applied after the solutions have been written down. It does not embed the admitted maps in a spacetime.

The cheapest objection is lexical. Dummett, in 1954 and again in 1964, treats the claim that a cause is earlier by definition as a stipulation about English. One can reserve the word for the earlier relatum and still ask whether a later event is necessary for an earlier one. Black's bilking argument, in Analysis in 1956, is the objection that survives. An agent who has seen the earlier event can try to prevent the later one. If the attempt succeeds, the later event was not necessary. If it cannot succeed, the agent lacks an ability the local facts appear to leave open. Horwich pressed the same point as a constraint: the theory must say what happens to the intervention.

Lewis, in 1976, splits the modal. Locally the attempt is possible. Globally it is not, because the past already contains the failure. That names the tension. It does not generate the failure. A process function generates it as non-admission. The attempt is a local function. If the composition has no fixed point, the pair is not a process. If it has two, the loop has not decided which history occurs.

2. Spacetime, and why measure zero is not a dynamics

Godel's 1949 solution has closed timelike curves through every event. Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever showed in 1988 that a traversable wormhole, held open by stress-energy violating the averaged null energy condition, can be converted into a time machine by relative motion of the mouths. Friedman and collaborators stated the changed Cauchy problem in 1990. Data admissible on a partial Cauchy surface can fail to extend, or can extend in more than one way. Novikov's principle assigns nonzero measure only to self-consistent solutions. Nothing reaches in to stop the traveller.

Echeverria, Klinkhammer and Thorne integrated the hard-sphere billiard and found data with one continuation, data with two, and data with none apparent. Carlini, Frolov, Mensky, Novikov and Solodukhin found the same ill-posedness from stationarity of the action. Earman's warning still holds. A condition imposed after the dynamics is not a dynamics. A process function wants multiplicity one for every intervention. The billiard does not supply it. That fact returns in section 7. It is the reason the standard wormhole is not an embedding.

3. Three quantum replies

Deutsch's 1991 model asks for a fixed point of the partial trace. Existence is a theorem in finite dimension. Uniqueness is not. The model is nonlinear and can clone. Aaronson and Watrous put the power at PSPACE. Bennett, Leung, Smith and Smolin called the power an artefact. Tolksdorf and Verch met the Deutsch condition with no closed curve. A Deutsch fixed point is not diagnostic of a loop.

Lloyd and collaborators replace the fixed point by post-selection. Inconsistent branches fail the post-selection. Process functions sit inside that class. The event list appears conditional on success. That is a simulation. It has the same shape as a measure-zero axiom.

Oreshkov, Costa and Brukner drop global causal order and keep local quantum mechanics. A process must return a probability for every instrument. The quantum switch does not violate a causal inequality. Some processes do. The formalism is not a claim that nature realises the violating processes.

4. Process functions

A process function is a map from outputs to inputs such that every local deterministic intervention has exactly one fixed point. Baumeler and Wolf described the classical polytope in 2016. Two parties are not enough for a classical causal inequality. Three are. The Lugano function, w(a,b,c) = (not b and c, not c and a, not a and b), is the witness. Each input depends on the other two outputs. All 64 binary interventions have one fixed point. The twelve binary two-party process functions are channels: four with no signalling, four one way, four the other.

No fixed point is the grandfather case. More than one is the bootstrap. The freedom in the quantifier is the no-new-physics principle of Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych. Any operation possible in an ordinary region remains possible. Consistency may not forbid the operation. It may only constrain the global solution. Dourdent, Leitherer, Boghiu, Simonov, Kunjwal and Acin restate the clause as a complete pairwise-exclusive event list, a multipartite form of Specker's principle. It characterises logical consistency. It does not say which lists embed in a spacetime.

5. Sketch of the proof

Reversibility is not free on the original alphabet. Lugano is two-to-one. Theorem 2 of the 2019 paper adds a source and a sink. The form used here is U|o, e> = |w(o)+e, o>. The inverse reads the sink as the output and the source as the difference. A bijection of a finite set is a permutation unitary. Validity for instruments is the extra claim.

Lemma. If w is a process function and s differs from s', some party k has s_k different from s'_k and w_k(s) = w_k(s'). Suppose not. Agreement of the function values already forces agreement of the strings. Define a local function that sends w_k(s) to s_k and w_k(s') to s'_k, and extend it off those points. Both strings are then fixed points of one intervention. A process function cannot have that. The swap is the witness the other way. The strings (0,1) and (1,0) under w(a,b) = (b,a) are both fixed by negation on each side.

Cancellation. Let each party apply a unitary V_k, possibly on a local ancilla. The source-to-sink amplitude is the product, over parties, of the amplitude for V_k to take the shifted source bit to the sink bit. Change variables by i_k = w_k(s)+e_k. The gram entry between s and s' factors into row overlaps against the shift delta_k = w_k(s')-w_k(s). If s = s', every shift vanishes and each factor is a row norm, hence 1. If s differs from s', the lemma supplies a party with shift zero and distinct row indices. That factor is the inner product of two distinct rows, hence 0. So the gram matrix is the identity. A local instrument is a unitary on a local ancilla followed by a partial trace. The partial trace of a unitary dilation is completely positive and trace-preserving. Fixing the source at the identity recovers w.

The same identity is why a non-process fails. The swap and the identity loop have distinct strings with no such party. Their dilations are not trace-preserving. The process-function clause is what kills the shift.

What was checked. lean/Full.lean, Lean 4.34.1, no unfinished proof: process_disagreement, the lemma on bits for every number of parties, and extend_left_inv, extend_right_inv, the bijection. lean/Lift.lean has exclusivity for an arbitrary alphabet. lean/Cancel.lean checks Lugano on all 64 interventions, and that the identity and the cycle fail. The sum, the change of variables as a theorem about the contracted operator, row orthonormality of a general complex unitary, and Stinespring are written.

6. Novelty

The result is not that Lugano has a unitary extension. Araujo, Feix, Navascues and Brukner showed that in 2017. Vanrietvelde, Ormrod, Kristjansson and Barrett wrote it as a routed circuit in 2022. The 2019 paper left the general quantum step open. Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal conjectured the general lift on 30 September 2026 and proved it under mutual exclusivity of control conditions and under unambiguity. This sketch does not use those conditions. The new step is that the forbidden double fixed point is the off-diagonal. The prohibition is checked. The identification with the gram matrix is written.

Only consistent loops occur can mean a filter, a post-selection, or a definition of the admitted set that is already closed under quantum instruments. The third reading is the one the sketch supports, if the written step is granted. Price argued in 2012 that a time-symmetric ontology should be retrocausal if the quantum state is real. Leifer and Pusey derived a timelike Bell factorization from no retrocausality, operational time symmetry, and lambda-mediation. Maudlin replied that the operational reverse is not always an experiment of the same kind. The dilation is adjacent. It removes the reply that the cyclic model dies when instruments become unitary. It does not settle the ontology.

7. Embeddability

A process function is a list of events. A spacetime is a metric. Nothing in the cancellation turns one into the other. Three things have been called an embedding. Post-selection is a simulation. The source and the sink are reversibility, not a throat. A Lorentzian embedding would be a local flow on a fixed metric with one solution for every intervention. Only the third is the gap.

The ordered case closes. The twelve binary two-party process functions are channels. A channel embeds in Minkowski spacetime as free propagation from the earlier party to the later one. No wormhole is required. A classical causal inequality needs three parties, so every two-party process function is of this kind.

The cyclic case does not. Lugano violates a causal inequality, so no definite order reproduces it if the settings are free. Vilasini and Renner show that indefinite causal order cannot be implemented with spacetime-localised systems in an acyclic spacetime without violating relativistic causality. The wormhole billiard has the wrong multiplicity. No other local Hamiltonian is known whose multiplicity is one for every intervention. The purification is a third object, beside the simulation and the embedding. The cyclic case stays open.

8. What is not claimed

Not a metric. Not a decision about freedom. Unique completion is not an open choice. Not a solution of the Leifer-Pusey no-go. Not a kernel-checked theorem that every process function purifies over the complex numbers. Not a refereed result. Unique completion answers the logical half of the demand in Faye's entry. It does not answer the geometric half.
