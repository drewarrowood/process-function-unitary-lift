## 2. Appendix G: two parties
Commits: 41f238c, 6a52d0c / 593c818, 5c717fd.
Every bipartite valid classical process, with any finite alphabets, is a blend of causally ordered process functions. The proof is term expansion followed by a positive split, the diagonal case of Oreshkov-Costa-Brukner 2012. With C.1, the purification conjecture holds for two parties with both sides true. LP vertex checks for alphabets up to (3,3,3,3) find only 0/1 vertices. Takeaway: the conjecture is a genuinely multipartite question. The result is known in substance; the purification corollary is stated here explicitly.

## 4. Appendix H: three parties
Commits: 921e6a3 / e23c7bd.
Proved: if some party is last (H.1), or some party is first with valid conditional bipartite processes (H.2), the process is a PF blend. So classically causally separable three-party processes satisfy the conjecture trivially. Being marginally first is not enough: E_ex1 is marginally first in every party. Failed: the single-vector determinantal bound for orbit 8, because the isotropic cone has rank-~51-55 points. Numerical: a unitary-side counterexample hunt found nothing; leaving the hull at Lugano costs purity at once. Takeaway: wherever a causal order exists, the conjecture is easy. What remains is the non-causally-separable region.

## 6. H.6: operator-level firstness
Commits: 3e99fae (H.6 first written) / see this log's integration commit.
Proved: if a purification V of W has a party first *in V* (its input prepared from the source alone; equivalently no output, including its own, influences it through V), then every conditional is a valid, purifiable bipartite process, so W is a PF blend (H.6.1 + H.2 + G.1). Numerics on the C.1 lifts (code/stochastic/structural/opfirst.py): in the H.5 blend, B and C are operator-first with valid conditionals and A is not; in Lugano no party is first, and by the contrapositive no purification of Lugano has one. Literature: close to Barrett-Lorenz-Oreshkov 2021 (Thms 3, 4), not found stated in this form. Takeaway: the open case is now purifications with no operator-first party.
