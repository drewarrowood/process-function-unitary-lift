Every process function lifts to a unitary process

Draft. Not submitted. Open extension is a bijection for every w. Closed contraction is not an isometry for every w.

Abstract

A causal loop is asked to do four jobs: not be a contradiction, not exclude contradictions by a rider about measure, be reversible if the laws are, and survive contact with quantum instruments. The grandfather case is an intervention with no fixed point. The bootstrap is an intervention with more than one. A process function is a map from outputs to inputs admitted only when every local intervention completes uniquely. Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych extend every such map to a bijection by a source and a sink. The closed contraction is unitary when the map is a process function, because a second fixed point would be a party who agrees in w and disagrees in the string, and that party is a zero factor. Conversely, if the map is not a process function, a copy gadget breaks unitarity, so the lift is unitary exactly when the loop is consistent. A three-party example then shows that reversible chancy loops are not just mixtures of deterministic consistent loops.

1. What the word cause is not doing

Dummett treated the lexical ban on backward causation as a stipulation about English. Black's bilking argument asks what happens to the intervention. Lewis's two cans dissolve a verbal paradox and do not say which histories the laws admit. Faye's entry still treats the consistency paradoxes as unresolved. The process-function clause is a criterion on maps.

The clerk and the closed window. Mara cannot hand in the form that would prevent the telegram. Each repair of the afternoon is a function with no fixed point. The story cannot be finished. That is the grandfather case, told without a grandfather.

2. The spacetime problem

G\u00f6del 1949 has closed timelike curves through every event. Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever convert a wormhole into a time machine if the averaged null energy condition fails. Friedman and coauthors state Novikov's principle. The Echeverria-Klinkhammer-Thorne billiard has one continuation, two, or none. Earman: a selection rule is not a dynamics. No Einstein metric for Lugano is constructed.

3. Three quantum replies

Deutsch closes a state. Tolksdorf and Verch mimic the fixed point with no curve. Aaronson and Watrous place the power at PSPACE. Lloyd and Maccone post-select. Oreshkov, Costa and Brukner write a process matrix. A process function closes a map.

4. Process functions

\(o_k = f_k(w_k(o))\) has exactly one solution for every local f. The identity loop and the swap fail. Lugano is the example that is not a causal order.

The note in the coat. Two wordings, two fixed points. The bootstrap is a failure of uniqueness. The line that differs and the line that agrees are the witness.

5. The reversible extension

\(U(o,e)=(w(o)\oplus e, o)\). Inverse \(U^{-1}(i,s)=(s, w(s)\oplus i)\). Both compositions are the identity by \(a\oplus(a\oplus b)=b\), for every w. Fixing e=0 recovers w. Recovery is not uniqueness.

6. The disagreement lemma

If w is a process function and s differs from s', some party k has s_k different from s'_k and w_k(s)=w_k(s'). Otherwise one local function fixes both. The witness need not be the first party.

7. The contraction

\(M_{s,e}=\prod_k \langle s_k|V_k|w_k(s)\oplus e_k\rangle\). This holds for every w. The process-function clause is used only when the off-diagonal must vanish.

The identity \(MM^\dagger=1\) and the validity chain for instruments are machine-checked in Lean 4 with Mathlib, standard axioms only (PFUL.induced_unitary, PFUL.valid_kraus, PFUL.valid_instrument, PFUL.Choi.validity_package), for the concrete contraction against complex local unitaries with ancillas. Countable alphabets: PFUL.Countable.induced_unitary_l2. What is checked and what is assumed is listed in lean/STATUS.md.

8. Unitary iff consistent

Theorem 1.2 of the technical note: the closed loop is unitary for every choice of local unitaries and finite ancillas if and only if w is a process function. The “if” half needs distinct rows to be orthogonal, which is what exclusivity gives; columns of length one would not be enough. The “only if” half is a copy gadget. If w is not a process function, some intervention f has N ≠ 1 fixed points. Let each party apply f and keep a copy of what it received in an ancilla, \(V_k|i,x\rangle=|f_k(i)+x,i\rangle\). The squared length of column (0,0) then counts the fixed points of f. It is N, not 1, so the loop is not unitary.

The ledger that counts histories. Every consistent history writes one line in the notebooks, and the copies keep the lines apart. Read the column and you have counted the histories. The grandfather case leaves the page blank, so probability is lost. The bootstrap writes two lines, so probability is counted twice.

Not verified: the converse without ancillas (only sampled), and infinite alphabets, which Theorem 1.2 does not cover.

9. Chance inside a loop

Purifiable means: produced by a pure process, with a fixed source state, each agent wire read in the computational basis, and the sink discarded. The frame must be named, because one pure process can give a mixture of process functions in one basis and a non-mixture in another. Mixtures of process functions are purifiable. The conjecture that nothing else is was refuted by Theorem H.12.1 of the technical note: three parties, one bit each, an explicit pure (unitary) Switchboard whose classical shadow lies outside the hull of the 744 process functions. It breaks a wall of the hull by exactly \(1/4-(3/16)\sqrt2\approx-0.0152\). Lean checks the statement; the inequality certifies an L1 margin of about 0.0076. The LP distance, 0.0607 in L1, is computed, not certified. H.21.2 identifies H.12 as a frame-rotated time reverse of the √X-Lugano process.

The referee's coin. A referee may toss a coin before the game to choose which consistent loop the players are in. Then the players' chance is only ignorance of the coin. The Switchboard has chance that no such coin explains. Consistent time loops are not just mixtures of deterministic consistent worlds.
