Unique completion, the unitary lift, and the embeddability gap

The essay, the equations, and the gap, in one page. Displays need no script. On bits, plus and minus are exclusive or.

What is shown. If a map has one fixed point for every local intervention, distinct output strings leave a party who disagrees in the output and agrees in the function value. On bits, the source-and-sink extension is a bijection. Both are in lean/Full.lean, Lean 4.34.1, no unfinished proof.

What is written. That witness kills the off-diagonal of the gram matrix, so the induced source-to-sink map is unitary. Instruments follow by a partial trace.

What is not claimed. A metric. A wormhole Hamiltonian for Lugano. A solution of the Leifer–Pusey no-go. Freedom. A refereed theorem.

1. The demand

Faye's Stanford entry, revised 28 October 2025, still treats the consistency paradoxes as unresolved. A logic of backward causation has to block grandfather cases and bootstrap cases without collapsing into the stipulation that only consistent loops occur. Dummett, in 1954 and in 1964, set the lexical objection aside. Black, in 1956, asked what happens to the intervention. Lewis, in 1976, split the modal and did not say which histories the laws admit. Horwich pressed the same point as a constraint. Earman, in 1995, said a filter imposed after the dynamics is not a dynamics.

A process function answers by a restriction on the admitted maps. An intervention is a tuple of local functions. The map is admitted only when the composition has one fixed point. No fixed point is the grandfather case. Two is the bootstrap. The identity loop fails the clause. The swap fails it. A rider that assigns inconsistent solutions measure zero cannot be failed from inside the theory in that way.

2. Spacetime

Gödel's 1949 solution has closed timelike curves. Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever, in 1988, convert a traversable wormhole into a time machine if the throat stays open. Friedman and collaborators stated the changed Cauchy problem in 1990. Novikov's principle keeps only self-consistent solutions. Echeverria, Klinkhammer and Thorne, in 1991, found billiard data with one continuation, data with two, and data with none apparent. Carlini, Frolov, Mensky, Novikov and Solodukhin found the same ill-posedness from the action. A process function wants multiplicity one for every intervention. The billiard does not supply it. Hawking's chronology protection is a different question: whether the curve forms.

3. Three quantum replies

Deutsch, in 1991, asks for a fixed point of the partial trace. Existence is a theorem in finite dimension. Uniqueness is not. Aaronson and Watrous put the power at PSPACE. Bennett, Leung, Smith and Smolin called the power an artefact. Tolksdorf and Verch met the Deutsch condition with no closed curve. Lloyd and collaborators replace the fixed point by post-selection. Process functions sit inside that simulation. Oreshkov, Costa and Brukner drop global order and keep local quantum mechanics. The quantum switch does not violate a causal inequality. Lugano does.

4. The equations

The fixed point is in the output:

o = f(w(o)). (1)

The Lugano function is

w(a, b, c) = (¬b ∧ c, ¬c ∧ a, ¬a ∧ b). (2)

Each input depends on the other two outputs. All sixty-four binary interventions have one solution of (1). The image has four elements out of eight, so (2) is two-to-one. The twelve binary two-party process functions are channels.

The source-and-sink unitary is

U |o, e⟩ = |w(o) + e, o⟩, (3)

with inverse

U−1 |i, s⟩ = |s, i + w(s)⟩. (4)

On bits the sum is exclusive or, so plus and minus are the same operation. Lean checked both compositions. A finite bijection is a permutation unitary. That is representation, not yet a quantum process. Dourdent, Leitherer, Boghiu, Simonov, Kunjwal and Acín restate (1) as a complete pairwise-exclusive event list, a multipartite form of Specker's principle. It characterises logical consistency. It does not say which lists embed in a spacetime.

5. Sketch of the proof

Lemma. If w is a process function and s ≠ s′, some party k has

sk ≠ s′k and wk(s) = wk(s′). (5)

Otherwise the local function that sends wk(s) to sk and wk(s′) to s′k fixes both strings in (1). The swap fails (5): under w(a, b) = (b, a), negation on each side fixes both (0, 1) and (1, 0). Lean accepted (5) as process_disagreement, on bits, for every number of parties.

Contract (3) with a local unitary Vk at each party. The amplitude is

Ms,e = ∏k ⟨sk| Vk |wk(s) + ek⟩. (6)

Change variables by ik = wk(s) + ek. The shift is

δk = wk(s′) − wk(s). (7)

The gram entry is the product of row overlaps against (7):

(MM†)s,s′ = ∏k ⟨row sk | row s′k shifted by δk⟩. (8)

If s = s′, every shift vanishes and each factor is 1. If s ≠ s′, (5) supplies a party with δk = 0 and distinct rows, so that factor is 0. Therefore

MM† = I. (9)

(9) is written, not kernel-checked. A local instrument is a unitary on an ancilla followed by a partial trace. Fixing the source at 0 in (3) recovers w. The integer rows (1, 1) and (1, −1) have dot product 0. A normalised Hadamard needs the square root of 2 and is not in the file.

6. Novelty

The result is not that (2) has a unitary extension. Araújo, Feix, Navascués and Brukner showed that in 2017. Vanrietvelde, Ormrod, Kristjánsson and Barrett wrote it as a routed circuit. The 2019 paper proves (3) classically and leaves the quantum step open. Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal conjectured the general lift on 30 September 2026 and proved it under two sufficient conditions this sketch does not use. The new step is that the forbidden double fixed point is the off-diagonal in (8). The prohibition is checked. The identification is written.

Price, Leifer and Pusey, and Maudlin are adjacent. A cycle drops an operational screening condition. It does not choose an ontology. Unique completion is not an open choice.

7. Embeddability

Three things have been called an embedding. Post-selection is a simulation. (3) and (4) are reversibility, not a throat. A Lorentzian embedding would be a local flow on a fixed metric with one solution of (1) for every intervention.

The ordered case closes. A channel is

iB = w(oA), iA independent of oB, (10)

or the reverse. (10) embeds as free propagation. No wormhole is required. A classical causal inequality needs three parties, so every two-party process function is of this kind.

The cyclic case does not. (2) violates a causal inequality, so no definite order reproduces it if the settings are free. Vilasini and Renner block an acyclic localized embedding. The billiard has the wrong multiplicity. No other local Hamiltonian is known whose multiplicity is one for every intervention. (3) supplies two extra regions, not a metric. The purification is a third object, beside the simulation and the embedding. The cyclic case stays open.
