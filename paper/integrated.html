Every process function lifts to a unitary process

One page. Each display is followed by what the symbols are and what the line is doing. On bits, addition is exclusive or. The kernel checked the bit case. The gram identity is written. A metric for Lugano is not claimed.

1. What is shown

Shown, Lean 4.34.1, no unfinished proof. If a process function sends two distinct output strings, some party disagrees in the output and agrees in the function value. On bits the source-and-sink map is a bijection, both ways. Lugano has one fixed point for every binary intervention. The identity loop and the three-cycle do not.

Written, not kernel-checked. The gram matrix of the source-to-sink operator is the identity. Instruments follow by Stinespring. Refused: nothing in the cancellation is a metric.

2. The fixed point

o = f(w(o)).

o is the string of outputs, one symbol from each party. w sends that string to the string of inputs the process returns. f is the intervention: at party k, a function from that party's input to that party's output. The equation says the outputs the parties emit are exactly what their own functions emit when they receive w(o). A solution is a history that survives the intervention. No solution is the grandfather case. Two solutions is the bootstrap. A process function is a w for which every f has exactly one solution. The clause can be failed. The identity loop fails it. The swap fails it.

3. Lugano

w(a, b, c) = (not b and c,   not c and a,   not a and b).

a, b, c are the three output bits. The first input is the conjunction of the negation of b and c, and the other two inputs are the cycle of that. Each input depends on the other two outputs, so no party is earlier than the others. All sixty-four binary interventions have one fixed point, which is why it is a process function. The image has four elements out of eight, so two outputs can share an input. Reversibility is not free on these three bits. That is the fact the source and the sink repair. Of 256 binary two-party maps, twelve are process functions and none is a cycle. A classical violation of a causal inequality needs three parties, so Lugano is the smallest witness.

4. The extension

(o, e) maps to (w(o) XOR e, o).

e is a source string, one bit per party, not part of the original alphabet. XOR is bitwise exclusive or, which is addition on bits. The first component is the input the process would have returned, shifted by the source. The second component is the output, copied into the sink. The map enlarges the alphabet so that a two-to-one function becomes a bijection.

(i, s) maps to (s, i XOR w(s)).

This is the inverse. s is the sink, which the forward map set equal to the output, so the output is recovered by reading s. The source is recovered by shifting the input i back by w(s). On bits, XOR cancels: applying one map and then the other returns the pair one started with. Lean checked both compositions. The ket form U of ket o, e equal to ket w(o) XOR e, o is the same map, written on the computational basis. A finite bijection is a permutation unitary. That is representation, not yet a quantum process. The 2019 paper proves the classical extension in general. The kernel check is the bit case.

5. The lemma

If w is a process function and s differs from s', some party k has s_k different from s'_k and w_k(s) equal to w_k(s').

s and s' are two output strings. The lemma says they cannot differ only where w also differs. Some party must be a witness: the outputs differ there, and the process returns the same symbol there. If not, the local function that sends w_k(s) to s_k and w_k(s') to s'_k is well defined, and both strings solve the fixed-point equation for that one intervention. A process function cannot have that. The swap fails the lemma in the other direction: under w(a, b) = (b, a), negation on each side fixes both (0, 1) and (1, 0). Lean accepted the construction for bits and for a general finite alphabet. This is the step the contraction uses.

6. The contraction, line by line

M(s, e) = product over k of the amplitude for V_k to take w_k(s) XOR e_k to s_k.

V_k is a unitary at party k, possibly on a local ancilla. The amplitude is the matrix element of that unitary from the shifted source bit to the sink bit. The product is the amplitude for every party to do this at once. M is the source-to-sink operator induced by the permutation unitary together with the local unitaries. Rows are indexed by the sink string s, columns by the source string e.

i_k = w_k(s) XOR e_k.

A change of variables. For a fixed output s, exclusive or with w_k(s) runs through every possible source bit exactly once as e_k does. The sum over sources can be rewritten as a sum over these i_k, one per party, with no Jacobian because the map is a bijection.

delta_k = w_k(s') XOR w_k(s).

The shift between two output strings, at party k. It is zero when the process returns the same symbol on both strings. It is one when the process returns different symbols. The lemma says that if the strings differ, some party has shift zero and distinct outputs.

The gram entry (M M-dagger) at s, s' is the product over k of the overlap between row s_k and row s'_k shifted by delta_k.

M M-dagger is the matrix one gets by summing the amplitude for s against the conjugate amplitude for s', over the source. After the change of variables the sum factors across parties. Each factor is an overlap of two rows of V_k, the second row shifted by delta_k. If s equals s', every shift is zero and each factor is a row norm, hence 1, so the diagonal of the gram matrix is 1. If s differs from s', the lemma supplies a party whose shift is zero and whose rows differ. That factor is the inner product of two distinct rows of a unitary, which is zero. One zero factor kills the product. Therefore

M M-dagger = I.

The induced map is unitary. A local instrument is a unitary on an ancilla followed by a partial trace, by Stinespring. The partial trace of a unitary dilation is completely positive and trace-preserving. Fixing the source at the zero string recovers w, because the input is then w(o) and the sink records o. Unitarity of the V_k does not by itself kill a nonzero shift. The process-function clause does, by forcing a party with shift zero. The swap and the identity loop fail the same contraction because they fail the lemma. This identification with complex local unitaries was not kernel-checked. The integer rows (1, 1) and (1, minus 1) have dot product 0. A normalised Hadamard needs the square root of 2 and is not in the file.

7. What the equations would close

If the gram identity stands, every classical deterministic process function purifies to a unitary process, and the dilation is the source-and-sink permutation itself. The 2019 question is then closed. The 2026 conjecture is a theorem, and the sufficient conditions of Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal are not the reason. Araujo, Feix, Navascues and Brukner quantized Lugano in 2017. That is one function. The sketch is a universal quantifier, conditional on the contraction. If the contraction does not stand, the page has the lemma and the bit bijection, and has not closed the conjecture.

8. Embeddability, explained the same way

i_B = w(o_A),   and i_A does not depend on o_B.

This is a channel. Party A is earlier. Party B's input is a function of A's output, and A's input does not depend on B. It embeds in Minkowski spacetime as free propagation from A to B, with the instrument at B. The twelve binary two-party process functions are all of this form, or the reverse, or a constant. No wormhole is required. Lugano is not of this form. Each of its inputs depends on the other outputs, and it violates a causal inequality, so no wiring in a definite order, and no mixture of orders, reproduces it if the settings are free.

Post-selection makes the Lugano event list appear in the runs that survive. That is a simulation. The probability of success is not one. The bijection embeds the function in a larger function. That is reversibility, and the extra coordinates are operational boundaries, not a throat. A Lorentzian embedding would be a local flow on a fixed metric with one solution for every intervention. The billiard of Echeverria, Klinkhammer and Thorne is a local Hamiltonian and has the wrong multiplicity: one continuation, two, or none. No other local Hamiltonian is known. Dourdent and collaborators leave the physical side open. This page does the same.

9. Not answered

Price, Leifer and Pusey, and Maudlin are adjacent. A cycle drops an operational screening condition. It does not choose an ontology, and it does not answer the no-go. Freedom is assumed, as a quantification over local functions, and not derived. Unique completion is not an open choice.
