Every process function lifts to a unitary process

A note on consistency, cancellation, and two closed loops that do not occur.
Repository: drewarrowood/process-function-unitary-lift. Expanded 6 October 2026.

Kernel boundary, stated once so it does not have to be smuggled into the prose. The disagreement lemma is checked for every finite number of parties on bits, and the source–sink bijection both ways. The gram identification and the any-index unitarity argument are checked in Lean 4.34.1, with no sorry and no axiom, for integer matrix entries and an arbitrary party list: the sum of a product equals the product of the sums, XOR with a fixed bit preserves the two-point sum, the source sum equals the product of shifted row overlaps, the diagonal is 1, and a disagreement witness at any index makes the off-diagonal entry 0. Complex conjugation is an involution on the second factor and is not moved by the reindexing; it is not a second compiled file. A spacetime embedding is not claimed. The instrument step — a local unitary on system plus ancilla, then a partial trace — is the usual dilation argument, not a formalised Stinespring theorem.
