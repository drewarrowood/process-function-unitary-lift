What was added, and what is still open

This page adds the items that were not in the first certificate, and says which of them are theorems.

Now checked

  • General finite alphabets. lean/Gaps.lean, theorem process_disagreement_alphabet, Lean 4.34.1, exit status 0. For any number of parties and any alphabet size, a process function cannot have two distinct output strings that agree in w wherever they differ.
  • Unnormalized Hadamard rows. hadamard_rows: the integer rows (1, 1) and (1, −1) have dot product 0. The normalized Hadamard, with a factor of one over square root two, is not in this scalar field.
  • A finite trace identity. trace_identity_ancilla is the statement that the two basis weights of an identity on a system, with a traced ancilla in the trivial case, sum to 1. It is not Stinespring.

Still not checked

  • The gram contraction of the permutation unitary against a general complex local unitary.
  • Stinespring as a theorem about completely positive maps.
  • Embeddability in a spacetime. SpacetimeEmbeddingClaim is defined as false in the file so that it cannot be used as a theorem. The wormhole billiard has the wrong multiplicity. No Hamiltonian embedding is claimed.

The bit-case bijection remains lean/Full.lean. The status note is paper/proved.html.
