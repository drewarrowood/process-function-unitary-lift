Unique completion, the lift, and the embeddability gap

About half again as long as the previous essay. Equations are set as equations. Claims stay marked.

Claims register. Shown, and kernel-checked on bits: exclusivity, and the source-and-sink bijection. Written: the gram cancellation and Stinespring. Not claimed: a metric, a wormhole Hamiltonian for Lugano, the Leifer–Pusey no-go, freedom.

1. The demand

Faye's Stanford entry, revised 28 October 2025, still treats the consistency paradoxes as unresolved. A logic of backward causation has to block grandfather cases and bootstrap cases without collapsing into the stipulation that only consistent loops occur. Dummett, in 1954 and in 1964, set the lexical objection aside. Black, in 1956, asked what happens to the intervention. Lewis, in 1976, split the modal and did not say which histories the laws admit. Horwich pressed the same point as a constraint on any theory that lets later events bear on earlier ones. Earman, in 1995, said a filter imposed after the dynamics is not a dynamics.

2. Spacetime

Gödel's 1949 solution has closed timelike curves. Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever, in 1988, convert a traversable wormhole into a time machine if the throat is held open by stress-energy that violates the averaged null energy condition. Friedman, Morris, Novikov and collaborators stated the changed Cauchy problem in 1990. Echeverria, Klinkhammer and Thorne, in 1991, found billiard data with one self-consistent continuation, data with two, and data with none apparent. Carlini, Frolov, Mensky, Novikov and Solodukhin derived a version of self-consistency from stationarity of the action and found the same ill-posedness. A process function wants multiplicity one for every intervention. The billiard does not supply it.

3. Three quantum replies

Deutsch, in 1991, asks for a fixed point of the partial trace. Existence is a theorem in finite dimension. Uniqueness is not. Aaronson and Watrous put the power at PSPACE. Bennett, Leung, Smith and Smolin called the power an artefact. Tolksdorf and Verch met the Deutsch condition with no closed curve. Lloyd, Maccone and collaborators replace the fixed point by post-selection. Oreshkov, Costa and Brukner drop global causal order and keep local quantum mechanics. The quantum switch, studied by Chiribella, D'Ariano, Perinotti and Valiron, and by Procopio and others in the laboratory, does not violate a causal inequality. Some processes do. Branciard, Araújo, Feix, Costa and Brukner gave the simplest causal inequality. Araújo and Feix found the Lugano function as a classical witness.

4. The objects

A party has an output and an input. A process function is a map \(w\) from output strings to input strings such that, for every tuple of local functions \(f\), the equation

$$o = f(w(o))$$

has exactly one solution. The Lugano function on three bits is

$$w(a,b,c) = (\neg b \wedge c,\ \neg c \wedge a,\ \neg a \wedge b).$$

Each input depends on the other two outputs. All 64 deterministic binary interventions have one fixed point. The twelve binary two-party process functions are channels. Dourdent, Leitherer, Boghiu, Simonov, Kunjwal and Acín restate the clause as a complete pairwise-exclusive event list, a multipartite form of Specker's 1960 principle. It characterises logical consistency. It does not say which lists embed in a spacetime.

5. The extension

Lugano is two-to-one. Theorem 2 of Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych (2019) adds a source \(e\) and a sink. The permutation used here is

$$U\lvert o, e\rangle = \lvert w(o)+e,\ o\rangle.$$

The inverse reads the sink as the output and the source as the difference. On bits, with addition as exclusive or, Lean checked both one-sided inverses. A bijection of a finite set is a permutation unitary. Validity for instruments is the extra claim.

6. Sketch of the proof

Lemma. If \(w\) is a process function and \(s \neq s'\), some party \(k\) has

$$s_k \neq s'_k \quad\text{and}\quad w_k(s) = w_k(s').$$

Otherwise the local function that sends \(w_k(s)\) to \(s_k\) and \(w_k(s')\) to \(s'_k\) fixes both strings. This is process_disagreement in lean/Full.lean, on bits, for every number of parties. lean/Lift.lean has it for an arbitrary alphabet.

Cancellation, written. Let each party apply a unitary \(V_k\), possibly on a local ancilla. The source-to-sink amplitude is a product of local amplitudes. Change variables by \(i_k = w_k(s)+e_k\). The gram entry factors:

$$(MM^\dagger)_{s,s'} = \prod_k \langle s_k \vert V_k\, T_{\delta_k} \vert s'_k\rangle,$$

where \(\delta_k = w_k(s')-w_k(s)\) and \(T\) is the shift. If \(s = s'\), every shift vanishes and each factor is a row norm. If \(s \neq s'\), the lemma supplies a party with \(\delta_k = 0\) and distinct rows. That factor vanishes. So \(MM^\dagger = I\). A local instrument is a unitary on an ancilla followed by a partial trace. That last sentence is Stinespring, and it is not in the kernel. The sum and the identification with the contracted operator are written. The witness party and the bijection are checked.

7. Novelty

Araújo, Feix, Navascués and Brukner quantized Lugano in 2017. Vanrietvelde, Ormrod, Kristjánsson and Barrett wrote that extension as a routed circuit. The 2019 paper left the general quantum step open. Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal conjectured the general lift on 30 September 2026 and proved it under mutual exclusivity of control conditions and under unambiguity. This sketch does not use those conditions. The new step is that the forbidden double fixed point is the off-diagonal. The prohibition is checked. The identification is written.

Price, in 2012, argued that a time-symmetric ontology should be retrocausal if the quantum state is real. Leifer and Pusey replaced that assumption with \(\lambda\)-mediation and derived a timelike Bell factorization. Maudlin replied that the operational reverse is not always an experiment of the same kind. The dilation is adjacent. It does not settle the ontology. Open quantum problem 43, on the list of open problems in quantum information, is not answered.

8. Embeddability

Post-selection is a simulation. The source and the sink are reversibility, not a metric. The twelve two-party process functions embed as Minkowski channels. Lugano violates a causal inequality, so no definite order reproduces it if the settings are free. Vilasini and Renner show that indefinite causal order cannot be implemented with spacetime-localised systems in an acyclic spacetime without violating relativistic causality. The wormhole billiard has the wrong multiplicity. No other local Hamiltonian is known whose multiplicity is one for every intervention. The cyclic case stays open.

9. What is not claimed

Not a metric. Not freedom. Unique completion is not an open choice. Not a solution of the Leifer–Pusey no-go. Not a kernel-checked theorem over the complex numbers. Not a refereed result. Unique completion answers the logical half of the demand in Faye's entry. It does not answer the geometric half.
