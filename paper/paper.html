Unique completion, the unitary lift, and the embeddability gap

Lean file. The checked half is lean/Full.lean. Lean 4.34.1 accepted the exclusivity lemma and the source-and-sink bijection on bits, with no unfinished proof. The gram identity is not in that file.

What is shown

If a map has one fixed point for every local intervention, distinct output strings leave a party who disagrees in the output and agrees in the function value. On bits, the source-and-sink extension is a bijection. Both are in the Lean file linked above.

Written, not checked: that witness kills the off-diagonal of the gram matrix, so the induced map is unitary. Instruments follow by a partial trace.

Not claimed: a metric, a wormhole Hamiltonian for Lugano, the Leifer-Pusey no-go, or freedom.

The demand

Faye's Stanford entry, revised 28 October 2025, still treats the consistency paradoxes as unresolved. Dummett set the lexical objection aside. Black asked what happens to the intervention. Lewis split the modal and did not select the histories. Horwich asked the theory to say what the intervention does. Earman said a filter after the dynamics is not a dynamics. A process function answers by non-admission. No fixed point is the grandfather case. Two is the bootstrap.

Equations

o = f(w(o)). (1)

No solution is the grandfather case. Two solutions is the bootstrap.

w(a, b, c) = (not b and c, not c and a, not a and b). (2)

Lugano. Each input depends on the other two outputs. All 64 binary interventions have one solution of (1). The image has four elements out of eight.

U sends (o, e) to (w(o) + e, o). (3)

The inverse sends (i, s) to (s, i + w(s)). (4)

On bits the sum is exclusive or. The Lean file checks both compositions.

If s differs from s', some party disagrees in the output and agrees in the function value. (5)

This is process_disagreement in lean/Full.lean. Otherwise one local function fixes both strings.

The shift is delta_k = w_k(s') - w_k(s). (6)

MM dagger = I. (7)

(7) is written. On the diagonal the shift vanishes. Off it, (5) supplies a zero factor.

A channel is i_B = w(o_A), with i_A independent of o_B. (8)

(8) embeds as free propagation. (2) has no such wiring if the settings are free. Post-selection is a simulation. (3) and (4) are reversibility, not a metric. The cyclic case stays open.
