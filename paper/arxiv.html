Unique completion as a quantization condition:
process functions, a unitary lift, and what is not a metric

A note in the manner of Foundations of Physics. Equations explained in the text. Claims separated.

Abstract. A process function is a classical map from the outputs of several parties to their inputs such that every local deterministic intervention has exactly one fixed point. Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych proved that every such map extends to a bijection by a source and a sink, and left open whether the bijection quantizes to a valid unitary process. Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal conjectured that it does. This note argues that the process-function clause is already the cancellation. If two output strings differ, some party disagrees in the output and agrees in the function value, and that party kills the off-diagonal of the source-to-sink gram matrix. The combinatorial half is kernel-checked on Lean 4.34.1. The identification with complex local unitaries is written and not kernel-checked. Ordered process functions embed as channels. The cyclic case, of which the Lugano function is the witness, has a simulation and a reversible extension, and no known local Hamiltonian embedding. The philosophical point is that a uniqueness clause which can be failed is not a stipulation that only consistent loops occur, and that reversibility of the boundary map is not a metric.

1. Introduction

Backward causation is asked to do four jobs. A loop should not be a contradiction. It should not exclude contradictions by a rider that cannot be failed from inside the theory. It should be reversible if the laws are. And it should survive the passage from Boolean interventions to quantum instruments. The literature has answers to each, and they are usually presented as one answer. They are not.

The first demand is logical. An earlier event that is the effect of a later one must still be the effect of that later one after one has described the intervention that would prevent the later one. The second is methodological. A principle that says only consistent solutions occur, and that assigns the inconsistent ones measure zero, cannot be failed by any experiment the theory itself counts as possible. The third is symmetry. If the laws do not distinguish a direction, the account of the loop should not smuggle a direction in by refusing to read the process backwards. The fourth is physical. Instruments are quantum.

This note keeps the four apart. Section 2 is the bilking argument and the reason a uniqueness clause is not Novikov's principle. Section 3 is the billiard, and the reason a unitary dilation is not a repair of it. Section 4 states process functions and writes the equations. Section 5 sketches the proof and says, under each display, what it is doing. Section 6 is embeddability. Section 7 is what is not claimed. The ledger is in section 8.

2. Bilking, without the stipulation

The linguistic objection is that a cause is, by definition, earlier than its effect. Dummett argued in 1954, and again in 1964, that this is a stipulation about the word, not a discovery about the world [1]. One can reserve the word for the earlier relatum and still ask whether a later event can be necessary for an earlier one. Mellor pressed the objection that a cause must raise the chance of its effect in a way a later event cannot, for an agent who has seen the earlier event [2]. The reply that matters here is not verbal.

Black's bilking argument is the better objection [3]. If an earlier event is supposed to be the effect of a later one, an agent who sees the earlier event can intervene so as to prevent the later one. Either the agent cannot intervene, which wants an explanation that is not itself the paradox, or the later event was not necessary for the earlier one. Lewis's reply splits the modal [4]. One can, relative to the local facts and the laws. One cannot, relative to the whole past. That dissolves a verbal paradox. It does not say which histories the laws admit. Horwich asks a theory of backward causation to say what happens to the intervention, not merely to rename the modal [5].

Faye's Stanford entry, substantively revised on 28 October 2025, still treats the consistency paradoxes as unresolved in this sense: a logic of loops has to block both the empty case and the overfull case without stipulating that only consistent loops occur [6]. The empty case is the grandfather paradox. An intervention is representable, and the composed map has no fixed point. The overfull case is the bootstrap. A trajectory, or a piece of information, is its own source, and the equations admit more than one continuation. Novikov's self-consistency principle blocks the empty case by refusing inconsistent data, and leaves the overfull case as a multiplicity of solutions [7]. A uniqueness clause blocks both. It can be failed. The identity loop fails it. The swap fails it. A rider that assigns inconsistent solutions measure zero cannot be failed from inside the theory. That is the methodological gain, and it is only a gain about maps.

3. The billiard is a different object

Godel's 1949 solution has closed timelike curves [8]. Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever showed that a traversable wormhole, if held open, can be converted into a time machine by relative motion of the mouths [9]. Friedman, Morris, Novikov, Thorne and collaborators proposed in 1990 that only self-consistent solutions occur [7]. Echeverria, Klinkhammer and Thorne integrated the hard-sphere billiard on the Morris-Thorne wormhole and found initial data with one self-consistent continuation, data with two, and data with none apparent [10]. Carlini, Frolov, Mensky, Novikov and Solodukhin derived a version of self-consistency from stationarity of the action and found the same underdetermination [11]. Earman's warning stands. A consistency condition imposed after the dynamics is not a dynamics [12]. Hawking's chronology protection conjecture is a different claim, that the curve does not form [13].

The billiard is the reason a unitary dilation is not an embedding. A process function demands exactly one fixed point for every intervention. The billiard has the wrong multiplicity. Deutsch's model keeps the multiplicity and averages it, at the cost of nonlinearity [14]. Post-selection throws the extra solutions away [15]. Neither is an embedding. A unitary process on a finite alphabet is a third object.

4. Process functions

Parties have finite alphabets. On bits the group law is exclusive or, and plus and minus agree with it. A process function w sends an output string to an input string. An intervention f is a tuple of local functions from a party's input to that party's output. The fixed point is in the output,

o = f(w(o)).   (1)

Equation (1) says the outputs the parties emit are what their own functions emit when they receive the inputs w returns. A solution is a history that survives the intervention. No solution is the grandfather case. Two solutions is the bootstrap. The map is a process function when every f has exactly one solution of (1). The freedom in the clause is the no-new-physics principle of Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych: any operation possible in an ordinary region remains possible [16]. Consistency may not forbid the operation. It may only constrain the global solution.

For two parties, consistency implies causal order. Of the 256 binary maps, twelve are process functions, and none is a cycle [17]. Four have no signalling, four signal one way, four the other. Baumeler and Wolf proved that a classical violation of a causal inequality needs three parties [17]. Every two-party process function is therefore a channel.

The Lugano function, found in the search of Araujo and Feix and written out by Baumeler and Wolf, is the three-party witness,

w(a, b, c) = (not b and c,   not c and a,   not a and b).   (2)

Each input depends on the other two outputs. All sixty-four binary interventions have one fixed point. The image has four elements out of eight, so (2) is two-to-one. Reversibility is not free on the original alphabet. Dourdent, Leitherer, Boghiu, Simonov, Kunjwal and Acin restate the clause as a complete and pairwise exclusive event list, a multipartite form of Specker's principle [18, 19]. Completeness and pairwise exclusivity are the unique fixed point, read as a list. The restatement does not mention a metric.

The source and the sink are the admission that a loop, read as a physical process, has a boundary. The extension and its inverse, on bits, are

(o, e) maps to (w(o) XOR e, o),   (3)

(i, s) maps to (s, i XOR w(s)).   (4)

Equation (3) shifts the input the process would have returned by a source string e, and records the output as the sink. Equation (4) recovers the output by reading the sink, and the source by shifting the input back. On bits, exclusive or cancels, so both compositions are the identity. Lean checked both. The ket form U of ket o, e equal to ket w(o) XOR e, o is (3) on the computational basis. A finite bijection is a permutation unitary. That is representation, not yet a quantum process. Theorem 2 of the 2019 paper is the general classical case [16].

5. Sketch of the proof

Lemma. If w is a process function and s differs from s', some party k has s_k different from s'_k and w_k(s) equal to w_k(s'). Otherwise the local function sending w_k(s) to s_k and w_k(s') to s'_k is well defined, and both strings solve (1) for one intervention. The swap fails the lemma the other way: under w(a, b) = (b, a), negation on each side fixes both (0, 1) and (1, 0). Lean accepted the construction for bits and for a general finite alphabet. This is the combinatorial half.

Contract (3) with a local unitary V_k at each party, possibly on an ancilla. The source-to-sink amplitude is the product, over parties, of the amplitude for V_k to take the shifted source symbol w_k(s) XOR e_k to the sink symbol s_k. The product is there because each unitary sees only its own symbol. Change variables by i_k = w_k(s) XOR e_k. Exclusive or with a fixed string is a bijection, so the sum over sources becomes a sum over these i_k. The shift between two output strings is

delta_k = w_k(s') XOR w_k(s).   (5)

Equation (5) is the only place the classical function enters the contraction. It vanishes when the process returns the same symbol on both strings. The gram entry is the product, over parties, of the overlap between row s_k of V_k and row s'_k shifted by delta_k. If s equals s', every shift vanishes and each factor is a row norm, hence 1. If s differs from s', the lemma supplies a party with shift zero and distinct rows. That factor is the inner product of two distinct rows of a unitary, and vanishes. One zero factor kills the product, so

M M-dagger = I.   (6)

Equation (6) is the claim that the induced map is unitary. A local instrument is a unitary on an ancilla followed by a partial trace [20]. The partial trace of a unitary dilation is completely positive and trace-preserving. Fixing the source at the zero string recovers w, because the input is then w(o) and the sink records o. Unitarity of the local operators does not kill a nonzero shift. The process-function clause does. The identification of (6) with a complex contraction was not kernel-checked. The integer rows (1, 1) and (1, minus 1) have dot product 0. A normalised Hadamard needs the square root of 2 and is not in the file.

If (6) stands, every classical deterministic process function purifies to a unitary process, and the dilation is (3) itself. The 2019 question is then closed. The conjecture of Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal is a theorem, and their sufficient conditions, mutual exclusivity of control conditions and unambiguity, are not the reason [21]. Araujo, Feix, Navascues and Brukner quantized Lugano in 2017 [22]. Vanrietvelde, Ormrod, Kristjansson and Barrett wrote that extension as a routed circuit [23]. Those are existence proofs for one function. The sketch is a universal quantifier, conditional on (6). If (6) does not stand, the note has the lemma and the bit bijection, and has not closed the conjecture.

6. Embeddability

Three things have been called an embedding. A post-selected circuit can make the Lugano event list appear in the runs that survive [15]. That is a simulation. The probability of success is not one. The source-and-sink bijection embeds the function in a larger function. That is reversibility. The 2019 paper realises it by interactions among bounded regions. It does not exhibit a metric. A Lorentzian embedding would be a local flow on a fixed metric whose solutions, for every intervention, are exactly the fixed points of the process function. Only the third is the gap.

The ordered case closes. A channel is i_B = w(o_A), with i_A independent of o_B, or the reverse. It embeds in Minkowski spacetime as free propagation from the earlier party to the later one. No wormhole is required. The cyclic case does not. Lugano violates a causal inequality [17, 22], so no wiring in a definite order, and no mixture of orders, reproduces it if the settings are free. A metric with closed timelike curves can, in principle. What is missing is a local Hamiltonian on such a metric whose consistency equation is (1) and whose multiplicity is one for every intervention. The billiard is a local Hamiltonian and has the wrong multiplicity [10]. No other one is known. Adding a source and a sink supplies two regions, not a throat. Dourdent and collaborators leave the physical side open on purpose [19]. This note does the same.

7. Adjacent arguments

Price argued that a time-symmetric ontology for quantum theory should be retrocausal [24]. Leifer and Pusey replaced reality of the quantum state with lambda-mediation and obtained a timelike Bell factorization, which sequential measurements violate [25]. Maudlin replied that the operational time-reverse of an experiment is not always an experiment of the same kind [26]. The dilation is adjacent to that argument, not a solution of it. A cycle already violates past-screening. Exhibiting a cycle does not answer the no-go. Freedom of choice is assumed, as a quantification over all local functions, and not derived. Unique completion is not openness of the choice. Purifiability of every extensibly causal process remains open [27]. Deutsch's computational power is PSPACE [28], and Bennett, Leung, Smith and Smolin argued that the power is an artefact of the fixed-point demand [29]. Tolksdorf and Verch showed that the Deutsch condition can be met with no closed timelike curve [30]. A Deutsch fixed point is not diagnostic of a loop. The quantum switch is causally nonseparable without violating a causal inequality [31]. Lugano violates one. The two phenomena are not the same.

8. Ledger

Shown: the disagreement lemma, any finite alphabet. Shown: both compositions of (3) and (4) on bits. Shown: (2) by enumeration, and the failure of the identity and the cycle. Written, not kernel-checked: (6), and the instrument step. Not claimed: a metric for (2), an answer to Leifer and Pusey, a settlement of freedom.
