Canonical unitary lift of a finite process function

Statement

The object of this note is one equivalence and one separation, both finite. They are the same mathematical statements as in the author’s public technical note [archive]. This file is an alternative write-up for a physics audience. It is not an independent discovery, and it does not survey the literature beyond the works cited for the definitions it uses.

Let each party kk have finite nonempty alphabets IkI_k and OkO_k, and write I=∏kIkI=\prod_k I_k and O=∏kOkO=\prod_k O_k. A map w=(wk):O→Iw=(w_k):O\to I is a process function when, for every tuple of functions fk:Ik→Okf_k:I_k\to O_k, the self-map o↦(fk(wk(o)))ko\mapsto(f_k(w_k(o)))_k of OO has exactly one fixed point [baumeler2016]. Identify each IkI_k with a cyclic group of order |Ik||I_k| when a sum in that alphabet is required. For every map ww, not only for process functions, the canonical extension is the basis permutation U|o⟩O|e⟩P=|w(o)+e⟩I|o⟩F.\label{eq:U} U\lvert o\rangle_O\lvert e\rangle_P=\lvert w(o)+e\rangle_I\lvert o\rangle_F. Let VkV_k be unitary from Ik⊗XkI_k\otimes X_k to Ok⊗YkO_k\otimes Y_k with |Ik||Xk|=|Ok||Yk||I_k|\,|X_k|=|O_k|\,|Y_k| and with Xk,YkX_k,Y_k finite-dimensional. The closed loop MM, from P⊗⨂kXkP\otimes\bigotimes_k X_k to F⊗⨂kYkF\otimes\bigotimes_k Y_k, has matrix elements M[(s,y),(e,x)]=∏kVk[(sk,yk),(wk(s)+ek,xk)].\label{eq:M} M\bigl[(s,y),(e,x)\bigr] =\prod_k V_k\bigl[(s_k,y_k),(w_k(s)+e_k,x_k)\bigr]. Call ww pure, for this extension, when every such MM is unitary. This is the purity condition of Araújo, Feix, Navascués, and Brukner, specialized to the family [eq:U] [araujo2017].

Theorem 1. The operator MM in Eq. [eq:M] is unitary for every local unitary and every matched finite ancilla if and only if ww is a process function.

Equation [eq:U] is unitary for every ww. Theorem 1 is therefore not the statement that a reversible encoding exists. It is the statement that the closed loop stays unitary under the whole family of local unitaries. The theorem does not say that an arbitrary dilation of an arbitrary channel is unitary only for process functions. The converse proved below uses one explicit family of local unitaries.

A second, separate statement uses a known board. It is a frame-rotated time reverse of a Lugano purification in the family of Guérin and Brukner, with one conditional bit flip replaced by a square root of that flip [guerin2018]. It is not a new device.

Theorem 2. There is a conditional distribution p(i∣o)p(i\mid o) on three binary parties, obtained by reading the computational basis of that board, such that L(p)=14−3162<0,\label{eq:L} L(p)=\frac14-\frac{3}{16}\sqrt{2}<0, while L≥0L\ge 0 on every three-bit process function in an enumeration recorded elsewhere. Hence pp is not a convex mixture of those process functions.

The sign of L(p)L(p) is proved in this file from Tables 1 and 2. The inequality L≥0L\ge 0 on process functions is not. Section 3 states the division of labor.

Proof of the equivalence

Lemma 3. If ww is a process function and s≠s′s\neq s' in OO, some party kk has sk≠s′ks_k\neq s'_k and wk(s)=wk(s′)w_k(s)=w_k(s').

Proof. Otherwise wk(s)=wk(s′)w_k(s)=w_k(s') implies sk=s′ks_k=s'_k for every kk. Define fk(x)=skf_k(x)=s_k if x=wk(s)x=w_k(s) and fk(x)=s′kf_k(x)=s'_k if x=wk(s′)x=w_k(s'), and define fkf_k arbitrarily on the rest of IkI_k. The two clauses agree where the inputs coincide. Then ss and s′s' are distinct fixed points of one tuple (fk)(f_k). ◻

Lemma 4. If ww is a process function, then MM is unitary for every choice of local unitaries and matched finite ancillas.

Proof. The Gram matrix of the rows is (MM†)[(s,y),(s′,y′)]=∏k∑ek,xkVk[(sk,yk),(wk(s)+ek,xk)]×Vk[(s′k,y′k),(wk(s′)+ek,xk)]¯.\label{eq:gram} \begin{split} &\bigl(MM^{\dagger}\bigr)\bigl[(s,y),(s',y')\bigr]\\ &\quad=\prod_k\sum_{e_k,x_k} V_k\bigl[(s_k,y_k),(w_k(s)+e_k,x_k)\bigr]\\ &\qquad\times\overline{V_k\bigl[(s'_k,y'_k),(w_k(s')+e_k,x_k)\bigr]}. \end{split} If wk(s)=wk(s′)w_k(s)=w_k(s'), the change of variables i=wk(s)+eki=w_k(s)+e_k makes the kkth factor the inner product of two rows of the unitary VkV_k. If s=s′s=s', every factor is of this kind and the product is δy,y′\delta_{y,y'}. If s≠s′s\neq s', Lemma 3 supplies a party whose factor vanishes. Thus MM†=1MM^{\dagger}=1. Matched dimensions make MM square, so MM is unitary. ◻

Column norms are not a substitute for this argument: unit-norm columns need not be orthogonal. Ancilla dimensions enter Lemma 4 only through squareness. The parties may share entanglement in the ancillas before the local unitaries act; the matrix element [eq:M] does not assume a product ancilla state. The state on PP is not part of the unitarity claim.

Proof of Theorem 1. Lemma 4 is one direction. Conversely, suppose a tuple ff has N≠1N\neq 1 fixed points. Take Xk=OkX_k=O_k, Yk=IkY_k=I_k, and Vk|i,x⟩=|fk(i)+x,i⟩.\label{eq:Vk} V_k\lvert i,x\rangle=\lvert f_k(i)+x,\,i\rangle. Each VkV_k is a permutation of a finite basis, hence unitary, and the dimensions match. The entries of column (0,0)(0,0) of MM are M[(s,y),(0,0)]=∏k[sk=fk(wk(s))][yk=wk(s)],M\bigl[(s,y),(0,0)\bigr] =\prod_k [s_k=f_k(w_k(s))]\,[y_k=w_k(s)], using Iverson brackets. The squared norm of that column equals the number of fixed points, which is N≠1N\neq 1. Hence MM is not an isometry. ◻

The local unitaries [eq:Vk] are diagonal in a shifted computational basis. The forward direction quantifies over every local unitary, including those that are not diagonal in that basis. Both directions are required for the stated equivalence.

Lemma 5. If MM is unitary for every local unitary on a matched finite ancilla, then every tuple of local finite-dimensional instruments induces a trace-preserving completely positive map from the source side to the sink side.

Proof. A local completely positive trace-preserving map dilates to an isometry on a finite environment [stinespring1955]. Pad both sides with finite junk spaces so the dilated map has equal finite input and output dimension, and extend the isometry to a unitary. Lemma 4 supplies a unitary on the enlarged loop. Discard the environments. Partial trace preserves complete positivity and, for a trace-preserving dilation, the trace. The link product is multilinear, so the original instruments yield a trace-preserving completely positive map. ◻

The padding step uses finite dimension. Nothing is claimed for continuous alphabets. With infinitely many parties, the exclusivity argument of Lemma 3 still yields at most one fixed point, while existence can fail. Whether every exclusive Baire-class-one self-map of {0,1}ℕ\{0,1\}^{\mathbb{N}} has a fixed point is open. That question is not used in Theorems 1 or 2.

Two binary examples fix the quantifiers in the definition, without adding a theorem. If one party copies a bit and the other flips it, no output pair is a fixed point. If the second party copies the bit, both values are fixed points. Neither map is a process function, while Eq. [eq:U] is still a permutation. The three-bit rule w(a,b,c)=(¬b∧c,¬c∧a,¬a∧b)w(a,b,c)=(\lnot b\land c,\lnot c\land a,\lnot a\land b) is a process function [baumeler2016]: every substitution of local bit functions has one history. For the three identity functions the history is (0,0,0)(0,0,0).

Witness in one basis

Mixtures of process functions are purifiable by a label. Prepare ∑jλj|j⟩\sum_j\sqrt{\lambda_j}\lvert j\rangle, apply the extension [eq:U] of the selected process function controlled on jj, and send the label to the sink. The loop is block diagonal with unitary blocks. Reading the computational basis and discarding the sink leaves the mixture. The converse is the content of Theorem 2, and only in a named basis.

For each output string o∈{0,1}3o\in\{0,1\}^3 let HH be the three-qubit Hadamard gate, the tensor product of three single-qubit Hadamard gates, and let mo(z)∈{0,1,2,3}m_o(z)\in\{0,1,2,3\} be Table 1, with zz in binary order from 000000 to 111111. Set Uo=Hdiag(imo(z))H.\label{eq:Uo} U_o=H\,\mathrm{diag}\bigl(i^{m_o(z)}\bigr)\,H. The source vector on the three qubits is ϕ=|011⟩+e−iπ/4|100⟩2,\label{eq:phi} \phi=\frac{\lvert 011\rangle+e^{-i\pi/4}\lvert 100\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}, and p(i∣o)=|⟨i|Uoϕ|2.\label{eq:p} p(i\mid o)=\lvert\langle i\rvert U_o\phi\rvert^2. The functional LL has integer coefficients giog_{io} in Table 2, rows ii and columns oo, and L(p)=∑i,ogiop(i∣o)L(p)=\sum_{i,o}g_{io}\,p(i\mid o).

Direct expansion of Eqs. [eq:Uo]–[eq:p] in the field ℚ(i,2)\mathbb{Q}(i,\sqrt{2}), followed by contraction with Table 2, produces Eq. [eq:L]. Equivalently, if each probability is written so that 128p(i∣o)128\,p(i\mid o) lies in ℤ[2]\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}], the same contraction gives ∑i,ogio128p(i∣o)=32−242,\sum_{i,o}g_{io}\,128\,p(i\mid o)=32-24\sqrt{2}, and dividing by 128128 is Eq. [eq:L]. Three further claims are not proved in this file.

Validity of pp as a classical process, meaning nonnegativity and normalization under every product of local stochastic maps, is a finite multilinear identity on the sixty-four deterministic local bit functions. The identity is recorded in the public repository [archive]. The terms are not expanded here.

Purity of the switchboard, in one route recorded in that repository, is the vanishing of 22322232 Pauli coefficients in ℚ(ζ8)\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_8), together with a lemma that finite ancillas do not create new failures for this shape of board. A second route reduces MM†=1MM^{\dagger}=1 to finitely many local slots. Neither expansion is copied here. Theorem 2 as a statement that pp comes from a pure process therefore depends on that external finite verification.

Nonnegativity of LL on three-bit process functions is an exhaustive check. The repository records 744744 such process functions, with L≥0L\ge 0 on each, the bound being tight on 418418 of them. This manuscript does not repeat the enumeration. A reader who does not grant it does not yet have, from this file alone, the proof that pp lies outside the convex hull. What this file proves unconditionally, given the tables, is the numerical value [eq:L].

The separation is basis-dependent in a precise sense. Read in the computational basis, the induced distribution is the pp above. The same pure process, read in another frame, need not leave the convex hull of process functions. No claim is made that the board is a new quantum process in every frame, or that L<0L<0 is a device-independent fact.

Finite tables of this size can be searched by enumerating output strings. That observation blocks any reading of Theorem 1 or Theorem 2 as a complexity separation or a hypercomputational claim. The search decides membership for an explicit table. It does not implement the wiring as a physical computer, and it does not transfer a runtime bound to a family the search was not given.

Relation to prior work

Baumeler and Wolf introduce process functions as the deterministic classical processes in which every choice of local functions has a unique fixed point [baumeler2016]. Equation [eq:U] is the reversible encoding used for that class. Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf, and Zych discuss reversible realization of deterministic classical processes compatible with arbitrary local operations inside a process theory, and they leave a general quantization of that bijection as a further question relative to the theory [baumeler2019]. Theorem 1 answers a narrower question: for this one encoding, unitarity of every closed loop under local unitaries and matched finite ancillas is equivalent to the process-function condition. It does not classify every unitary dilation.

Araújo et al. define a pure process by unitarity of the loop under local unitaries [araujo2017]. Theorem 1 says which classical rules meet that definition for the canonical extension. It does not say that purity restores a mixture of process functions. Theorem 2 is a counterexample in one basis, and only once the external nonnegativity check is granted. Oreshkov, Costa, and Brukner introduce the process-matrix framework in which these loops are written [oreshkov2012]. The two-party classification, that valid classical processes are mixtures of causally ordered process functions, is not re-proved here. The witness uses three parties because that is where the cited separation lives.

No speedup, query complexity, or spacetime embedding is stated. Equation [eq:U] is not a physical realization. A negative value of LL in one basis is not a claim about the ontology of the process in every basis.
