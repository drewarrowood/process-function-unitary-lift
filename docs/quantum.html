The canonical unitary lift of a finite process function

Contributions

The main result is Theorem 6: for finite alphabets, and for the canonical extension UU defined in Eq. [eq:U], the closed loop is unitary for every local unitary and every finite ancilla of matching dimension if and only if the classical rule is a process function. Theorem 5 is the forward half. The witness in Theorem 8 is a separation conv(PF)⊊Purif\operatorname{conv}(\mathrm{PF})\subsetneq\mathrm{Purif} in one named basis. Assumptions are finite nonempty alphabets, finite-dimensional ancillas, and the link product written in Eq. [eq:M]. The board in Theorem 8 is not new; it is a frame-rotated time reverse of a known Lugano purification [guerin2018].

Definitions

Parties k∈{1,…,n}k\in\{1,\ldots,n\} have finite nonempty alphabets IkI_k and OkO_k. Write I=∏kIkI=\prod_k I_k and O=∏kOkO=\prod_k O_k. For the permutation form, identify IkI_k with the cyclic group of order |Ik||I_k|.

Definition 1 (Process function). A map w=(wk):O→Iw=(w_k):O\to I is a process function when, for every tuple of functions fk:Ik→Okf_k:I_k\to O_k, the self-map o↦(fk(wk(o)))ko\mapsto (f_k(w_k(o)))_k of OO has exactly one fixed point [baumeler2016].

A fixed point is a history for that intervention. Acyclic deterministic circuits are process functions. They are not all of them. The Lugano rule w(a,b,c)=(¬b∧c,¬c∧a,¬a∧b)w(a,b,c)=(\lnot b\land c,\lnot c\land a,\lnot a\land b) is cyclic and is a process function [baumeler2016; araujo2017]. For the identity intervention the only solution is (0,0,0)(0,0,0). If a=1a=1, then b=0b=0 and c=1c=1, but the third coordinate requires c=0c=0.

Definition 2 (Canonical extension). For every map w:O→Iw:O\to I, not only for process functions, U|o⟩O|e⟩P=|w(o)+e⟩I|o⟩F.\label{eq:U} U\lvert o\rangle_O\lvert e\rangle_P=\lvert w(o)+e\rangle_I\lvert o\rangle_F. UU is a permutation of a finite basis, hence unitary [baumeler2019].

Definition 3 (Closed loop). Let VkV_k be unitary from Ik⊗XkI_k\otimes X_k to Ok⊗YkO_k\otimes Y_k with |Ik||Xk|=|Ok||Yk||I_k|\,|X_k|=|O_k|\,|Y_k| and Xk,YkX_k,Y_k finite-dimensional. The closed loop MM from P⊗⨂kXkP\otimes\bigotimes_k X_k to F⊗⨂kYkF\otimes\bigotimes_k Y_k has M[(s,y),(e,x)]=∏kVk[(sk,yk),(wk(s)+ek,xk)].\label{eq:M} M\bigl[(s,y),(e,x)\bigr] =\prod_k V_k\bigl[(s_k,y_k),(w_k(s)+e_k,x_k)\bigr].

Following Araújo, Feix, Navascués, and Brukner, call the process pure when every such MM is unitary [araujo2017]. A classical process is purifiable when it is induced by a pure process from a source state by measuring in a stated orthonormal frame and discarding the sink. Unless a frame is named, the frame is the computational basis.

The equivalence

Lemma 4 (Exclusivity). If ww is a process function and s≠s′s\neq s' in OO, some party kk has sk≠s′ks_k\neq s'_k and wk(s)=wk(s′)w_k(s)=w_k(s').

Proof. Otherwise wk(s)=wk(s′)w_k(s)=w_k(s') implies sk=s′ks_k=s'_k for every kk. Define fk(x)=skf_k(x)=s_k if x=wk(s)x=w_k(s) and fk(x)=s′kf_k(x)=s'_k if x=wk(s′)x=w_k(s'), and define fkf_k arbitrarily elsewhere. The two clauses agree on the overlap. Then ss and s′s' are distinct fixed points of one intervention. ◻

Theorem 5 (Unitary lift). If ww is a process function, then for every choice of local unitaries and matched finite ancillas, MM is unitary.

Proof. For rows (s,y)(s,y) and (s′,y′)(s',y'), (MM†)[(s,y),(s′,y′)]=∏k∑ek,xkVk[(sk,yk),(wk(s)+ek,xk)]Vk[(s′k,y′k),(wk(s′)+ek,xk)]¯.\label{eq:gram} \bigl(MM^{\dagger}\bigr)\bigl[(s,y),(s',y')\bigr] =\prod_k\sum_{e_k,x_k} V_k\bigl[(s_k,y_k),(w_k(s)+e_k,x_k)\bigr]\, \overline{V_k\bigl[(s'_k,y'_k),(w_k(s')+e_k,x_k)\bigr]}. If wk(s)=wk(s′)w_k(s)=w_k(s'), the substitution i=wk(s)+eki=w_k(s)+e_k makes the kk-th factor the inner product of two rows of the unitary VkV_k. If s=s′s=s', every factor is of this kind and the product is δy,y′\delta_{y,y'}. If s≠s′s\neq s', Lemma 4 supplies a party whose factor vanishes. Thus MM†=1MM^{\dagger}=1. The dimension hypothesis makes MM square, so MM is unitary. ◻

Column norms are not a substitute: unit-norm columns need not be orthogonal. The ancilla dimensions enter Theorem 5 only through squareness.

Theorem 6 (Canonical equivalence). Let w:O→Iw:O\to I be any map between finite alphabets, with each IkI_k a cyclic group as above. The closed loop MM is unitary for every local unitary and every matched finite ancilla if and only if ww is a process function.

Proof. The forward direction is Theorem 5. Conversely, suppose an intervention ff has N≠1N\neq 1 fixed points. Take Xk=OkX_k=O_k, Yk=IkY_k=I_k, and Vk|i,x⟩=|fk(i)+x,i⟩V_k\lvert i,x\rangle=\lvert f_k(i)+x,\,i\rangle. This is a permutation, hence unitary. The entries of column (0,0)(0,0) of MM are M[(s,y),(0,0)]=∏k[sk=fk(wk(s))][yk=wk(s)],M\bigl[(s,y),(0,0)\bigr] =\prod_k [s_k=f_k(w_k(s))]\,[y_k=w_k(s)], so the squared norm of that column equals NN. Hence MM is not an isometry. ◻

The converse uses one family of unitaries. It does not say that an arbitrary dilation is unitary only for process functions. Equation [eq:U] is unitary for every ww. The parties are allowed to insert unitaries that are not diagonal in the computational basis. That is the test Theorem 6 quantifies over.

Lemma 7 (Instruments). If MM is unitary whenever every local map is a unitary on a matched finite ancilla, then the induced process is valid under every tuple of local finite-dimensional instruments.

Proof. A local completely positive trace-preserving map dilates to an isometry [stinespring1955]. Pad with finite junk spaces so both sides of the dilated map have equal finite dimension, and extend the isometry to a unitary. Theorem 5 gives a unitary on the enlarged loop. Discard the environments. Partial trace and a fixed ancilla state preserve complete positivity and the trace, and the link product is multilinear, so the original instruments yield a trace-preserving completely positive map from the source side to the sink side. ◻

The argument uses finite dimension to complete the isometry. It is not a claim about continuous alphabets or about arbitrary infinitely many parties. With infinitely many parties, exclusivity still yields at most one fixed point, while existence can fail. Whether every exclusive Baire-class-one self-map of {0,1}ℕ\{0,1\}^{\mathbb{N}} has a fixed point is open. That question is not used in Theorems 5–8.

A distribution outside the hull

Mixtures of process functions are purifiable. Prepare a control register in ∑jλj|j⟩\sum_j\sqrt{\lambda_j}\lvert j\rangle, apply the reversible extension of the selected process function controlled on jj, and route the control to the sink. The loop is block diagonal with unitary blocks. Reading the computational basis and discarding the sink leaves the mixture.

Theorem 8. There is a valid classical process pp on three binary parties, induced in the computational basis by a pure process with a finite source, such that L(p)=14−3162<0,\label{eq:L} L(p)=\frac14-\frac{3}{16}\sqrt{2}<0, while L≥0L\geq 0 on every three-bit process function. Thus conv(PF)\operatorname{conv}(\mathrm{PF}) is a proper subset of the purifiable classical processes in this sense.

Three checks are distinct, and they are not equally self-contained in this text.

Validity of pp, meaning nonnegativity and total probability one under every product of local stochastic maps, is a finite multilinear identity on the sixty-four deterministic local functions, extended by multilinearity. The identity is an exact finite check in the technical note. The arithmetic is not expanded here. This is a gap in the present write-up, not a gap in the claim’s intended proof: the check is finite and has been recorded elsewhere, but a reader of this file alone cannot inspect every term.

Purity of the switchboard is, in one archive route, the vanishing of 22322232 Pauli coefficients in the cyclotomic field ℚ(ζ8)\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_8), together with a lemma that finite ancillas do not create new failures for this shape of board. Those coefficients are not listed here. A second archive route reduces MM†=1MM^{\dagger}=1 to finitely many local slots by multilinearity. Neither expansion is copied into this manuscript. Theorem 8 therefore depends, for purity, on that external finite verification. The dependence is flagged so that it is not mistaken for a proof written above.

Non-membership in the convex hull, given pp, is the sign of LL. Table 2 is the integer kernel, copied from the formalization’s table gg, with rows ii and columns oo. The board is not a new quantum process in every frame. Up to a local frame it is a time-reversed variant of a Lugano purification in the family of [guerin2018], with one conditional bit flip replaced by a square root of that flip. Read in the computational basis, the induced classical process lies outside the hull. Read in another basis, a pure process of this family can induce a mixture. The separation is about a named frame.

Nonnegativity of LL on process functions is a finite check of the 744744 binary three-party process functions. The count 744744 is the exhaustive enumeration of the definition on three bits in the technical note. This manuscript does not repeat the enumeration. A reader who does not grant that enumeration does not yet have, from this file alone, the proof that L≥0L\geq 0 on every process function. The case split used in the formalization is: either five relevant output strings already make L≥0L\geq 0, or one of four explicit interventions has two fixed points among them. That case split is not re-encoded here. The gap is the same kind as the purity gap: finite, external, and not rhetorical.

Relation to prior work and limits

[baumeler2019] give a reversible realization of deterministic classical processes compatible with arbitrary local operations, and leave the general quantization of that bijection as a further question relative to the process theory. Theorem 6 is a quantization statement for one canonical extension and one class of tests, not for every unitary dilation. [araujo2017] define purity as unitarity under local unitaries. The lift says which classical rules meet that definition for UU. It does not say that purity restores a mixture of process functions. Theorem 8 is the counterexample in one basis. It is not a new causal inequality and not a speedup theorem. No complexity class is claimed. Finite explicit tables can be searched by enumerating output tuples. That fact is not a uniform simulation theorem and not a spacetime realization.

Two-party valid classical processes are mixtures of causally ordered process functions; the interesting separation uses three parties [oreshkov2012]. The present text does not re-prove the bipartite classification.
